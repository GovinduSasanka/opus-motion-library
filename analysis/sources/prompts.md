# Prompt corpus study — what people ask coding agents to animate

Expansion round 2, source research (see `docs/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`). Generated by `out/prompt-research/parse_cases.py` and `out/prompt-research/render_md.py` (run with `python -I`, outside the source folders) from three read-only collections; every case is in `analysis/sources/prompt-cases.json` (records) and `docs/prompt-index.json` (case → our motions).

| Source | Licence | What we used | Records |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `awesome-opus5-5-videos` | MIT | `data/videos.json` + `prompts/*.md` (category, remake tech, prompt analysed in memory) | 703 |
| `awesome-claude-5-5-videos` | CC BY 4.0 | `data/collection-index.csv` (all listed posts) + review ledgers, `domain-style.csv`, thumbnails | 424 |
| `opus-video-prompts` | none | `cases.json` (54) + 18 prompt files → our own titles/summaries only, no prompt text kept | 58 |

**1138 unique cases** after merging posts that appear in more than one collection. Prompts are mostly short (median ~220 characters in the MIT set), so technique counts below are *lower bounds*: a 'make a dynamic 15 s motion graphics video' prompt names no technique at all. Techniques are found with a keyword taxonomy (`out/prompt-research/taxonomy.py`, English + Chinese/Japanese terms) plus techniques implied by a stated or labelled style. Treat counts as indicative.

### What the 61 sampled thumbnails show

Contact sheets of 61 case thumbnails from `awesome-claude-5-5-videos/assets/*` (CC BY 4.0), stratified over 16 clusters, were built with ffmpeg into `analysis/frames/prompt-thumbs/` (git-ignored, index in `index.txt`) and inspected. Recurring visual facts:

- Launch/promo frames are UI-first: real app screens inside a watch/phone/browser frame, a cursor on the UI, floating notification cards over a pale gradient with a serif question, giant cropped words bleeding off the frame with tiny mono metadata in the corners (light editorial) or a dark green-black UI with a soft glow (dark minimal).
- Explainers are diagram-first: glowing amber wiring diagrams on navy, a calculus curve with labelled extrema on slate, encoder/decoder boxes with an equation (3b1b grammar), warm cream infographic cards with progress bars and bilingual captions, step-by-step IKEA-like instruction UI.
- 3D cases are cinematic: golden-hour sea and pier, photoreal river aerials, a vinyl turntable product shot, a claymation owl with a serif caption, low-poly figures in a desert.
- Stories use flat vector night towns, risograph-grained window views, anime cel frames with subtitles, a theatre-curtain silhouette frame, paper-cut rooms lit by a spotlight circle, a plush 3D cat.
- Music videos: karaoke lyric line with the sung word highlighted under an aurora, manga speed-line burst with huge Japanese type, 80s outrun car title card, collision-music machine on black, organic teal fractal.
- History: Renaissance painterly room with a serif era label, a doodle notebook timeline with era props, a vertical flat poster with a year ruler across the top.
- Data: vertical sports player card (cut-out portrait, stat tiles, one yellow highlight), giant green counter on black for a token dashboard, Japanese comparison cards with progress bars.
- Retro/pixel: rainbow pixel runner, rainy pixel Paris, neon voxel corridor at speed; generative: ceramic mosaic goldfish, dense dot fields, red ring lattices; kinetic type: 'FAST' with a red accent letter over horizontal speed streaks plus mono HUD corners.

These added three items to the taxonomy (`profile-stat-card`, `split-gameplay`, styles `rubber-hose` and `retro-sunburst`) and confirmed the karaoke and corner-metadata details used below.

## 6. Top 20 recurring visual styles

Evidence = cases that state the style in the request + cases labelled with it in the CC BY 4.0 snapshot; `styles.json` already has the ones marked EXISTS. New ones should be added as style-engine defs (`src/styles/defs`: SVG filter steps + overlays + `step`), so they restyle every motion; the signature moves are the motions that make the look read best. Fonts are Google Fonts. Palettes are ours.

### 1. style `dark-tech-minimal` — Dark tech minimal
- essence: Near-black canvas, white type, one hot accent; UI and type do all the work; hairlines and mono metadata in the corners. (evidence: 47 stated, 67 stated-or-labelled cases)
- palette: bg #0A0A0B, surface #141416, ink #F5F5F4, muted #8A8A8E, line #2A2A2E, accent #FF5A1F (or brand), accent2 #3B82F6
- texture: none, or 3-5% animated grain; soft 40-80 px glow only behind the hero UI
- type: Geist / Inter Tight (display, tracking -3%), Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono (metadata, timecodes)
- motion: 60 fps feel, critically damped springs (<=3% overshoot), masked reveals, cuts on the beat, no bounce
