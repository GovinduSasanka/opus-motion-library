## Motion techniques

### `voxel-rain-build` — NEW
- look: a voxel model assembles itself from cubes that fall from above the frame and land in their slots. Cubes spawn as a loose rain column (lots in flight at once), each lands with a tiny settle; the model grows from the bottom up (legs → body → arms → top rows). The eye holes are just black voxels in the grid.
- params: 1200 voxels in ≈ 2.75 s (0.50→3.25). Placement rate starts slow (≈ 75/s for the first 0.5 s, legs) then is roughly linear ≈ 560/s, i.e. `placed(t) = N·easeInQuad` for the first 15 % then linear. Each cube falls ≈ 0.9–1.1 H in ≈ 0.45–0.55 s with **easeInQuad** (gravity), lands with a 2–3 frame settle (scale 1.06 → 1, no bounce higher than 0.1 voxel). Cubes in flight are slightly smaller (0.85×) and semi-transparent near the top edge (they fade in over the first 6 frames). Spawn x/z jitter ±0.15 voxel, small random spin (≤ 20°) that resolves to 0 on landing. Order: sort voxel slots by `y` (layer), shuffle within a layer (seeded), then stagger `start_i = buildStart + rank_i / rate`.
- build: three.js `InstancedMesh` of a rounded box (bevel 0.08) with `MeshStandardMaterial` (roughness 0.6) and a soft `DirectionalLight` + `HemisphereLight`, `PCFSoftShadowMap` on a shadow-catcher plane; or a pure-SVG isometric fallback (3 faces per cube, painter's sort). Model = a list of integer cells `{x,y,z,color}` (built-in `box-bot` mascot, or `cells` from a tiny text format / MagicaVoxel `.vox` converted to JSON). Per frame: `u = clamp((f − start_i)/fallFrames)`, `y = target.y + dropH·(1 − u²)`… written as `y = target.y + dropH − dropH·easeInQuad(u)`; settle `s = 1 + 0.06·sin(π·clamp((f − land_i)/3))`. Props: `cells` | `preset`, `voxelSize`, `buildStart`, `buildFrames`, `order` (`bottom-up` | `random` | `radial`), `dropHeight`, `palette`, `eyeColor`, `seed`.
- source: x1 0.25–3.30 s (`b_build.png`, `sheet.png`).
- value: "assembling the product / feature / team" beat; a loader that builds a logo or mascot; brandable when `cells` come from a logo bitmap.

### `voxel-burst-swirl` — NEW (with reverse use as an intro)
- look: the finished model shatters into its cubes, which fly outward and tumble into a thick ring/donut around the plinth, orbit it for a beat, then the whole cloud rises and accelerates out of the top of frame. The black eye voxels stay visible inside the cloud, so you can still "read" the character for a moment.
- params: **burst** 7.25→≈7.75 s: radial (mostly horizontal) explosion, cloud radius grows from 0.5× to ≈ 2.2× body half-width with easeOutCubic; vertical spread ±0.35 H; every cube gets a random tumble (angular speed 90–360°/s) and stays full size; gaps open within 2 frames. **swirl** 8.0–9.25: cloud orbits the plinth's vertical axis ≈ 40–60°/s (front cubes move left→right), slow bob ±10 px; inner radius ≈ 0.6 body width so the plinth shows through the hole. **lift** 9.25–≈11.0: all cubes rise with easeInCubic (top cubes first by ≈ 0.3 s), reaching > 1.2 H above start; the cloud's shadow on the plinth fades with it; the last cube clears the top edge ≈ 11.0. Counter jumps to 0000 at the burst frame (not counted down).
- build: per voxel seeded `θ_i` (around the plinth), `r_i ∈ [0.7, 1.3]·R`, `h_i ∈ [−0.35, 0.35]·H`, spin axis `a_i`, spin rate `w_i`. Position = lerp(home_i, ringPos_i(θ_i + orbitSpeed·t), easeOutCubic(burstU)) then `+ (0, liftH·easeInCubic(liftU_i), 0)` with `liftU_i` delayed by `−0.3 s·(y_i/H)`. Rotation = `w_i·t` about `a_i`. Run the same function backwards for a "cloud gathers into the mascot" intro. Props: `burstAt`, `burstFrames`, `orbitFrames`, `orbitSpeed`, `liftFrames`, `ringRadius`, `ringHeight`, `tumble`, `exit` (`lift` | `fall` | `scatter` | `reassemble`).
- source: x1 7.2–11.1 s (`b_burst.png`, `b_lift.png`).
- value: dramatic "disrupt / transform / remix" moment; a loop's outro that returns to the empty stage; data turning into particles.

### `voxel-hop-spin` — EXTENDS `box-bot` (new action `hop-spin`; same curve useful for `blob-mascot`)
- look: the mascot crouches (squash), springs up stretched, does a full 360° turn around its vertical axis at the top of the hop, lands with a squash and settles. Shadow on the plinth shrinks and lightens while it is airborne.
- params (top-edge tracking at 30 fps): anticipation 4.00→4.18 s, body drops ≈ 0.14 H with **scaleY ≈ 0.88, scaleX ≈ 1.06** (easeInOut); launch 4.18→4.32 (easeOutQuad) to apex ≈ 0.10 H above rest with scaleY 1.08 (stretch); hang 4.32→4.52; **yaw 0→360° over 4.25→4.65** (easeInOutCubic, so the side and back faces show at the top); fall 4.52→4.72 (easeInQuad); land squash 4.72 (scaleY 0.90); recover 4.72→4.92 with one small overshoot (1.02). Total ≈ 0.92 s.
- build: in `box-bot`'s action table add `hop-spin: {anticipate: 11 f, rise: 8 f, hang: 12 f, fall: 12 f, recover: 12 f @60}` with `yaw = 360·easeInOutCubic(...)`. Shadow: `scale = 1 − 0.4·height/apex`, `opacity = 0.5 − 0.25·height/apex`. Props: `apex`, `spin` (deg), `squash`, `durationFrames`.
- source: x1 3.85–4.95 s (`b_hop.png`, `hop/` measurements).
- value: a cheerful "ta-da" for a mascot after it appears; reaction to good news.

### `voxel-eye-blink` / `voxel-limb-wave` — EXTENDS `box-bot` (voxel render of existing `blink` / `wave` actions)
- look: blink = the 2×4-voxel black eye slots collapse to a 1-voxel-high line in the middle row and reopen; wave = the stub arm (a 2×2×3 voxel block) swings around its shoulder pivot.
- params: blink close 3 frames, closed 4 frames, open 4 frames (60 fps), at 3.55 and ≈ 6.6 s. Wave: amplitude 0→45° around the z-axis at ≈ 3 Hz for ≈ 1 s, while the whole body yaws ±6° in counter-phase. Look: body yaw ±15° (easeInOutSine, 0.6 s each way).
- build: eyes are a separate voxel group whose `scaleY` steps 1 → 0.25 → 1 (quantised to whole voxels: hide the top and bottom rows); arm = group pivot at the shoulder. Props: reuse `box-bot` actions; add `render: 'voxel'`.
- source: x1 3.25–7.25 s (`b_hop.png`, `b_wave.png`).
- value: character life between big beats; keeps a loop from feeling dead.

### `plinth-progress-ring` — NEW
- look: the round display plinth has a ring of ≈ 120 small notches near its top edge. As voxels are placed, the notches light up orange one by one around the ring (a radial progress bar that is part of the set). They stay lit while the model exists and drain back to grey during the lift.
- params: plinth diameter 0.47 W, top ellipse aspect ≈ 0.34 (camera ≈ 20° above the plinth plane), side band 0.05 H. Notches: ≈ 120, each ≈ 0.6 % W long × 0.4 % W wide, inset 4 % of radius from the edge; unlit `#C9C1B8` (a slightly darker dent), lit `#F47A2D`. Lit fraction = placed / total; fill direction clockwise from the front-left. Drain during lift: lit fraction = 1 − liftProgress (finished ≈ 10.9 s).
- build: SVG or three.js: notch `k` lit if `k/N < progress`; on an elliptical ring `x = cx + r·cos(φ)`, `y = cy + r·sin(φ)·aspect`; draw the far half before the model and the near half after it. Props: `progress` (0..1, or bind to a counter), `count`, `color`, `idleColor`, `diameter`, `aspect`.
- source: x1 0.5–3.3 s and 9.3–11.0 s.
- value: progress or loading indicator that lives in the scene (product pedestals, "unlocking" a reward, onboarding steps).

### `voxel-count-hud` — EXTENDS `hud-chrome` (new `phaseBadge` + `counter` slots)
- look: minimal dev HUD: top-left a zero-padded counter "0817 / 1200 VOXELS" with a grey sub-line; top-right a small rounded badge naming the current phase (READY in black, every action in orange) and a frame counter "029 / 720" under it. The badge text swaps instantly on phase change (no animation); its width hugs the word.
- params: see Overview > Fonts/Layout. Counter is zero-padded to 4 digits and updates every frame. Phase list: READY, BUILD, BLINK, HOP, WAVE, LOOK, BURST, SWIRL, LIFT, READY.
- build: `hud-chrome` slots: `counter: {value, total, unit, pad: 4}`, `subline`, `badge: {text, color}` driven by a `phases: [{from, label, color}]` array, `frameCounter: true`. Overlay-capable.
- source: x1 whole video (`badge_strip.png`).
- value: "made in code" honesty layer for demos, explains what the motion is doing; any step-labelled loop.

### `studio-plinth-stage` — NEW (backdrop)
- look: seamless warm studio backdrop with a soft vignette, a round matte plinth with a bevelled top edge and notch ring, one soft key from the upper left so contact shadows fall right/back. A "product turntable" stage for any 3D subject.
- params: backdrop radial `#F3F1ED` (centre at 0.5 W, 0.35 H) → `#E2E1DA` corners; plinth centred at x 0.5 W, top at 0.59 H, diameter 0.47 W; shadow blur ≈ 3 % H, opacity ≈ 15 %, offset to the right ≈ 0.08 W.
- build: three.js scene preset in `three-kit` (`stage: 'plinth'`): `CylinderGeometry` with a small chamfer, `ShadowMaterial` floor plane, camera FOV 30°, elevation 20°, distance so the plinth fills 47 % W. SVG fallback: two ellipses + side band gradient + blurred shadow ellipse. Props: `bg`, `plinthColor`, `ring` (see above), `cameraElevation`, `orbit` (deg/s, default 0).
- source: x1 whole video.
- value: neutral stage for products, logos, mascots and 3D charts.

## Camera

Locked-off, ≈ 20° above the plinth plane, long lens (little perspective: the 13-voxel front face keeps near-parallel verticals). No orbit and no zoom; all perceived motion comes from the subject (the mascot's yaw during HOP/LOOK shows its sides). The HUD is screen-space.

## Styles

### style `voxel-studio` — Voxel clay studio
- essence: matte toy-like voxels on a warm, seamless studio set with one soft light and big soft shadows; a single saturated accent colour against warm greys; quiet, product-shot calm.
- palette: bg `#F3F1ED` (vignette to `#E2E1DA`), paper/plinth `#E2DCD7`, ink `#040300`, muted ink `#87837D`, accent (voxels, badges, progress) `#F47A2D` / `#FF6623`, accent shade `#904739`, accent2 (eyes, details) `#050301`.
- texture / post: no grain, no outlines. Rounded cube bevel (8 % of edge), AO darkening in crevices ≈ 10 %, soft shadow (PCF, radius ≈ 3 % H). In the pixel-style engine: `bloom 0`, `vignette 0.12`, very slight warm grade (`temperature +4`).
- typography: **JetBrains Mono** (all HUD: counters, badges, sub-lines; uppercase, +5–10 % tracking). If a title is needed: **Inter Tight SemiBold**.
- motion rules: 60 fps smooth; physical easing (gravity easeInQuad falls, easeOutCubic explosions, squash/stretch with one small overshoot); phases of ≈ 0.75–1.25 s each, named in the HUD; loop seamlessly by returning to the empty stage.
- signature moves: `voxel-rain-build`, `voxel-hop-spin`, `voxel-burst-swirl`, `plinth-progress-ring`, `voxel-count-hud`, `studio-plinth-stage`.
- do: keep one accent colour and let shading give the depth; show the count; keep the subject centred on the plinth. don't: add outlines or textures, use more than one saturated hue, cut the camera.
- source: x1 (video by someone else; analysis only).
