## Motion techniques

### `paper-diorama` — NEW
- look: a landscape built from 6–10 flat paper cut-out layers at different depths, each casting a soft shadow on the layer behind, with deckled light rims on torn edges, paper fibre texture and a blurred near prop. A hero character stands on the mid layer. The whole stack breathes with a slow parallax dolly, small ambient loops (water, stars, fireflies, rain) and the character's idle.
- params: layer depths roughly sky 0, clouds 0.1, far range 0.2, near range 0.35, forest 0.5, mid/hero 0.65, near rocks 0.85, foreground prop 1.0. Inter-layer shadow: offset (0, +0.6 % H), blur 0.8 % H, `#000` at 35–45 %. Torn rim: 2–4 px light line (`#F2E9E4` at 70 %) on top edges only. Near-layer DOF blur ≈ 6–10 px at 1080p. Hero size 0.24 H (fox: ear tip to feet), placed on a third (x 0.25–0.43 W, feet at y ≈ 0.55 H).
- build: a `PaperLayer` component = `<g filter="url(#paper)">` around an SVG path or image; filter = `feTurbulence`(fibre, baseFrequency 0.9, 2 octaves) → `feDisplacementMap` scale 1.5 on the alpha (rough edge) → `feMorphology dilate 1` + `feFlood` light colour for the rim (composite `out` against the shape, offset −1 px y) → `feDropShadow` dy 0.6 %H, std 0.8 %H. Layers as an array `{src|path, depth, blur?}` sorted back to front; the camera transform per layer is `scale = 1 + zoom·depth`, `translate = pan·depth` (see `multiplane-dolly`). Procedural defaults: scalloped clouds (circle unions), faceted mountains (polygons with a lighter left facet), pine rows, a stream ribbon. Props: `layers[]`, `palette`, `hero` (`src` or built-in paper fox), `heroAction` (`walk` | `sit` | `look-up`), `ambient` (`stars` | `fireflies` | `rain` | `aurora` | `water`), `guideStar`, `dolly`, `seed`.
- source: x5 every scene (0–59 s).
- value: storybook / brand-journey films, children's education, calm website hero loops, "chapter" backgrounds where text sits in the sky band.

### `popup-layer-rise` — NEW
- look: the scene assembles like a pop-up book: layers slide up from below into place, far layers first, the nearest last and from furthest down; the sky is already there.
- params (phase-correlation, 1080p): far mountains rise ≈ 95 px (8.8 % H) over 0–0.6 s; mid (fox, meadow) ≈ 90 px over 0.33–1.1 s; foreground flowers ≈ 200 px (18.5 % H) over 0.33–1.2 s. Per-layer curve **easeOutCubic** (per-frame step 45 → 36 → 10 → 3 → 1 px). Stagger ≈ 0.15–0.3 s back to front; travel ∝ depth (`rise = 0.09H + 0.1H·depth`). No overshoot.
- build: `y_i = rise_i · (1 − easeOutCubic(clamp((f − start_i)/dur_i)))`, `start_i = i·stagger`, `dur_i ≈ 36–48 f at 60 fps`. Reverse it (easeInCubic, near first, downward) for an exit. Props: `stagger`, `duration`, `riseBase`, `risePerDepth`, `direction` (`up` | `down`).
- source: x5 0.0–1.2 s (and reversed/forwarded inside `layer-swap` 47.6–48.3 s).
- value: an "opening the book" intro for any layered illustration; product exploded-view reveals; slide build-ins.

### `multiplane-dolly` — EXTENDS `ken-burns` (new `layers` + `depthZoom` props)
- look: the slow push-in of every scene: near layers grow and slide outward faster than far ones, so the still illustration feels deep.
- params: scene 2, measured over 4.5 s: far moon/sky region drifts (+6, −4) px/s, near rocks (+16, +4) px/s, near-left flowers (−5, +7) px/s → zoom about a focus near (0.42 W, 0.40 H), **far ≈ +0.8 %/s, near ≈ +2 %/s**, linear (no visible ease within a 6 s scene). Scene 1 adds a lateral truck: foreground −27 px/s, mid −6 px/s, sky 0.
- build: in `ken-burns` accept `layers: {src, depth}[]`; per layer `scale = 1 + rate·t·(0.4 + 0.6·depth)`, `x += pan·t·depth`, origin = `focus`. Props: `layers`, `rate` (%/s at depth 1), `pan` (px/s at depth 1), `focus`.
- source: x5 6.5–11.0 s (measured), all scenes.
- value: turns layered art (or an image split into depth layers) into a living backdrop with zero authoring.

### `guide-star-ignite` — EXTENDS `star-glint` (new `trail` props)
- look: a four-point golden star bursts on in the sky with a bloom flash and a spray of tiny sparkles, settles, then a dotted trail of 6 tiny stars pops on one by one behind it, pointing back down towards the hero, and twinkles. In later scenes the same star sits in the sky with a slow pulse.
- params: ignite 1.53 s: bloom radius 0.06 W → settles to a 0.042 W star in ≈ 0.17 s (easeOutQuad), 8–12 sparkle specks fly 0.03 W outward and fade. Star at (0.65 W, 0.075 H). Trail 1.87–2.20 s: 6 stars, 0.06 s apart, sizes 0.012 W → 0.006 W (smaller away from the star), along a gentle arc down-left, spacing 0.025 W. Twinkle: scale 0.85–1.1 at 1.2 Hz, random phase. Colour `#D6B65D` with `#FFF3C4` core and soft glow.
- build: reuse `star-glint` with `spikeAngles=[0,90,180,270]`; add `trail: {count, spacing, angle, curve, stagger, sizeFrom, sizeTo}` where each trail star pops with `spring({damping: 14})`. Props: `x`, `y`, `size`, `trail`, `twinkle`.
- source: x5 1.2–3.4 s (`b_star.png`); star motif in every scene.
- value: "follow your goal" beats, a brand mark that leads the viewer through chapters, CTA attention points.

### `firefly-swarm` — EXTENDS `embers` (new mode `fireflies`)
- look: 40–60 warm glowing dots wander slowly through a dark forest, each trailing a short faint comet tail, pulsing on and off at different rates; denser near the light source.
- params: dot radius 3–6 px (1080p) with 18–30 px glow, colour `#FFE08A` core / `#F2B33D` glow; tails 15–40 px fading to 0; drift 20–40 px/s on smooth noise paths; blink period 1.5–3 s (soft, 30 % of the time dim). Spread over the mid-ground band (y 0.15–0.75 H).
- build: per firefly seeded noise path `p(t) = p0 + A·(noise(t·0.3+seed))`; tail = 6 previous positions drawn with decreasing opacity; `mix-blend-mode: screen`. Props: `count`, `color`, `tail`, `blink`, `band`.
- source: x5 12.2–17.6 s (`amb_ff.png`).
- value: magical / night / "ideas floating" atmosphere; overlay on any dark scene.

### `paper-critter-rig` — NEW
- look: a paper-cut animal (here a red fox) made of overlapping scale-shaped paper pieces, which can walk in place, sit, swish its tail and lift its head to look at the star. Edges keep the paper rim and a contact shadow.
- params: walk cycle in place ≈ 0.9 s per stride (legs alternate, body bob 1 % of body height); sit idle: tail swish ±8° at 0.4 Hz, head tilt up 6–10° every 2–3 s with easeInOutSine, ear flick 2 frames. Fox palette above.
- build: SVG rig of parts (`body`, `head`, `ears`, `tail` 3 segments, 4 legs), each a cut-out path filled with a scale pattern (overlapping ellipse "feathers", 2 tones) + the `paper-diorama` filter. Pivots: neck, tail root, hips/shoulders. Pose sets: `walk`, `sit`, `stand`, `look-up`. Props: `species` (`fox` default; recolour to `cat`/`wolf`), `pose`, `colors`, `facing`.
- source: x5 2–6 s (walk, `amb_fox.png`), 12.5–17.5 s (sit idle, `amb_fox2.png`).
- value: a reusable storybook hero for explainer and kids' content; mascot in a paper world.

### `portal-glow` — NEW
- look: a golden ring standing in stone ruins, a four-point star in its centre, light rays slowly rotating behind the star, a glow pool below on a basin, small glints along the pillars; the ring pulses brighter until it blooms white (transition).
- params: ring diameter 0.12 W, stroke 0.006 W `#EDDE9D` with 0.03 W glow; 16–24 rays rotating 6°/s; pulse 0.9–1.0 opacity at 0.5 Hz; final bloom ramps 0.25 s easeInExpo.
- build: SVG group of ring + star + `<path>` ray fan with radial gradient mask, `transform: rotate(t·6°)`; screen-blend glow circles. Props: `x`, `y`, `size`, `color`, `rays`, `pulse`, `bloomAt`.
- source: x5 48.3–53.9 s (`amb_portal.png`), also the cave ripple glow 30.4–35.8 s.
- value: "the goal / destination / unlock" beat; a hero element for launches and level-ups.

### `foreground-occluder` — NEW (transition)
- look: a near-camera prop from the scene's world (tree trunk, fern fronds, a row of trunks, a cloud bank, a wall of waves) passes in front of the lens and hides the cut; the next scene is revealed behind its trailing edge. The prop is softly blurred (DOF) and casts a shadow edge, so it feels like part of the diorama, not a wipe.
- params: duration 0.5–0.85 s (x5: 0.50 trunk, 0.65 foliage, 0.80 trunks, 0.67 clouds, 0.87 waves); full cover holds 0–0.35 s; travel easeInQuad for sweeps, easeOutCubic in / easeInCubic out for close-and-part. Blur 8–14 px at 1080p. The prop extends 0.25 W beyond the trailing edge with a ragged silhouette (fronds, scallops, crests).
- build: like `wipe`, but the mask edge is the prop's silhouette: draw old scene, then the new scene masked to the region behind the trailing edge, then the prop on top (`<g filter="blur">`). Variants: `trunk` (1 trunk + fronds, direction), `trunks` (N trunks, depth-scaled widths and speeds), `foliage` (frond sprites from 4 edges → flat leaf colour), `clouds` (scalloped bands from top), `waves` (sine crests with foam rims from bottom), `custom` (`src` of any PNG/SVG). Props: `variant`, `direction`, `color`, `blur`, `holdFrames`, `src`.
- source: x5 5.67, 11.50, 17.60, 23.58, 41.55 s (`b_5_7.png`, `tr_*.png`).
- value: in-world transitions for illustrated, storybook, travel and nature films; much richer than a flat wipe.

### `rock-iris` — EXTENDS `iris` (variants `shape`, `centerOnSubject`, two-phase close/open)
- look: the classic cartoon iris-out, but the aperture is an irregular ring of paper rocks (a tunnel mouth) on dark felt, centred on the hero; it closes to black and reopens on the next scene's hero position.
- params: see the table above; aperture edge = 14–20 overlapping polygon "rocks" `#5C5A5A`–`#9A958F` with a 1 px light rim; surround `#3C3736` vignetting to `#080805`.
- build: add `variant: 'rock'` to `iris`: radius curve close → hold black → open; `centerFrom` / `centerTo` so the aperture lands on the subject in each scene; aperture drawn as a seeded ring of polygons; same single-render mask approach as `iris`. Props: `centerFrom`, `centerTo`, `holdFrames`, `variant` (`circle` | `rock` | `custom` with `src`).
- source: x5 29.58–30.40 s (`tr_29.5.png`, `f_29.7.png`).
- value: a classic storybook "the end of this chapter" beat that stays in the paper style; a cave or tunnel passage.

### `layer-swap` — NEW (transition)
- look: the diorama is taken apart and rebuilt. The old scene's layers drop out of frame **near-to-far** (foreground, then fox and bridge, then lake, then mountains), with vertical motion blur, until only the sky is left; the sky cross-fades to the new sky; the new layers then rise in **far-to-near** (portal and ruins, then lake, then fox on its rock, then foreground flowers).
- params (30 fps burst, 47.55–48.33): exit 47.60–47.85 (≈ 0.25 s, about 2 frames per layer at 60 fps, easeInCubic, drop distance 0.3–0.6 H, motion blur along y); sky cross-fade 47.80–47.98; entry 48.05–48.33 (≈ 0.3 s, easeOutCubic, rise 0.15–0.35 H, near layers last). Total ≈ 0.73 s.
- build: needs both scenes as layer stacks (`paper-diorama` or any `{layers}` motion). For layer i of N: `exitStart = i·stagger` (i = 0 nearest), `y = dropH·easeInCubic(u)`; `enterStart = exitEnd + skyFade + j·stagger` (j = 0 farthest), `y = riseH·(1 − easeOutCubic(u))`. Fake the blur with `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 v"` ∝ velocity. Props: `stagger`, `dropH`, `riseH`, `skyFadeFrames`.
- source: x5 47.55–48.33 s (`b_layerswap.png`).
- value: the signature "pop-up book page turn" for layered scenes; explaining that a system is rebuilt from components.

## Camera

- Never a cut, never a handheld shake. Each scene is one continuous multiplane dolly (linear, ≈ 1–2 %/s on near layers) — see `multiplane-dolly`.
- Scene 1 adds a lateral truck (foreground −27 px/s) while the fox walks in place, giving "travelling" without moving the hero.
- Transitions are where the camera "moves" between scenes: past trunks (truck), into clouds (tilt down), under waves (tilt up), through a tunnel (iris).
- The hero always sits on the left or centre-left third, facing right, looking up at the star (upper right), so the eye line runs from fox to star in every scene.

## Styles

### style `paper-diorama` — Paper-cut diorama
- essence: a storybook world of layered cut paper with soft shadows between layers, torn light rims and fibre texture; deep, cosy, handmade, lit by one warm guide light.
- palette: bg night `#182C4B` / dusk `#3C4E8C`, sky warm `#D9A290`, glow `#EAC59C`, ink (dark shadow) `#2A1F15`, paper rim `#F2E9E4`, mountain `#5E65A8`, foliage `#2D4C36`, meadow `#AEA54C`, water `#6091D2`, accent (hero) `#C95523`, accent2 (star / gold) `#D6B65D`, accent3 (magic glow) `#EDDE9D`.
- texture / post: per layer, not per frame — paper fibre via `feTurbulence` (baseFrequency 0.8–1.0, numOctaves 2) multiplied at 6–10 %; edge roughness `feDisplacementMap` scale 1–2; light top rim (`feMorphology` + offset −1 px, `#F2E9E4` 60–70 %); inter-layer `feDropShadow` dy 0.6 % H, std 0.8 % H, 40 %. Whole frame: mild vignette 15 %, slight warm bloom on light sources (screen-blend radial gradients), DOF blur on the nearest layer only. As a pixel-only style in `src/styles` (for motions that are not layered): `saturate 1.1` → `posterize 12` → `edges` in `#F2E9E4` width 1 at 30 % → paper overlay (`paper: {color: '#EDE4D6', amount: 0.08, fiber: 0.6}`) → vignette 0.15; it approximates the look but lacks the layer shadows.
- typography: none in the source. When text is needed: **Fraunces** (soft serif, titles, in the sky band) and **Nunito** (captions); or text cut out as a paper layer with the same rim/shadow.
- motion rules: 60 fps smooth, no stepped timing; slow linear dollies inside a shot; layer-based entrances (pop-up rise, far first) and exits (drop, near first); easeOutCubic in / easeInCubic out; transitions are in-world occluders every 6 s; ambient loops (stars, fireflies, water, rain) always on but dim; the hero idles, never freezes.
- signature moves: `paper-diorama`, `popup-layer-rise`, `multiplane-dolly`, `guide-star-ignite`, `paper-critter-rig`, `foreground-occluder`, `layer-swap`, `rock-iris`, `whiteout:burnout`.
- do: keep one warm light source per scene; keep the hero on a third with the eye line to the light; dim ambient particles; let near layers blur. don't: put hard black outlines on shapes, use flat untextured fills, cut hard between scenes, or use more than one transition type in a row.
- source: x5 (video by someone else, analysis only; no licence to reuse imagery).

## Library candidates

- `paper-diorama` — layered storybook scene engine (new motion, environment family) with built-in paper filter.
- `popup-layer-rise` — pop-up-book build-in for any layered scene (core helper + prop on layered motions).
- `multiplane-dolly` — `ken-burns` `layers` + depth-scaled zoom.
- `guide-star-ignite` — `star-glint` `trail` prop for a guiding star.
- `firefly-swarm` — `embers` mode `fireflies`.
- `paper-critter-rig` — paper-cut animal character (fox default) with walk / sit / look-up.
- `portal-glow` — golden ring portal with rotating rays and bloom.
- `foreground-occluder` — new transition with `trunk`, `trunks`, `foliage`, `clouds`, `waves`, `custom` variants.
- `rock-iris` — `iris` variant with a rock-ring aperture, subject-centred close/open.
- `layer-swap` — new pop-up-book transition between layered scenes.
- style `paper-diorama` — new style (craft family) + a `PaperLayer` wrapper for per-layer shadows/rims.
