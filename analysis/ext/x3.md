## Motion techniques

### `book-open-3d` — NEW
- look: a closed hardback lies on a desk at a 3/4 top view. The front cover swings open around the spine, showing endpapers / a contents page; one or two leaves turn over (each bending slightly as it turns), and the book settles open on a two-page spread while the camera pushes in and levels out.
- params: cover rotation 0 → 180° over 0.40–0.75 s (easeInOutCubic; it passes vertical at ≈ 0.60), cover thickness ≈ 1.5 % of the book width, cast shadow sweeping across the mat; page turns 0.65–1.0 and 0.9–1.25 (≈ 0.35 s each, overlapping), leaf bend: the free edge lags the spine by ≈ 15° at mid-turn; camera dolly-in ≈ 25 % and tilt from ≈ 50° to ≈ 62° elevation over 0.4–1.6 s (easeOutCubic). Book ≈ 0.6 W wide closed, 0.95 W open.
- build: three.js: cover = box hinged at the spine; each leaf = a `PlaneGeometry` (20×1 segments) whose vertices are bent with `angle(x) = θ·(1 − 0.15·sin(π·x))` around the spine; page textures = SVG rasterised to canvas (title, contents, lesson). SVG/2.5D fallback: `scaleX = cos θ` with skew for the cover and leaves, and a shadow polygon. Props: `coverColor`, `coverTitle`, `coverArt` (src), `pages[]` (src or text), `turns`, `openAtFrame`, `camera` (`from`, `to`), `desk` (`wood`, `matColor`).
- source: x3 0.30–1.60 s (`t_open.png`, `b_a.png`).
- value: textbook, storybook, "chapter 1" openers; education products; any brand story told as a book.

### `lineart-popup-extrude` — EXTENDS `plan-tilt` / `wireframe-build` (new `popup` mode: flat page drawing → 3D ink model)
- look: the flat line drawing printed on the page rises off the paper into 3D: the shelf uprights stand up first, the shelf planks swing up, jar outlines float up as ellipses and then extrude into cylinders, and the small objects (bike, mug, battery, magnets, balloon, spring) lift from their flat drawings into line models. During the rise everything is just black ink lines (no fill), so it reads as "the drawing comes alive".
- params: 1.60–2.60 s total; stagger back → front and big → small: uprights 1.60–1.80, planks 1.80–2.05, jar rims appear 1.90 and extrude 2.05–2.40, objects 2.20–2.55; each piece easeOutBack(1.2) on its rise (≈ 0.25 s, ≈ 5 % overshoot); line weight ≈ 1.5 px constant in screen space; camera keeps pushing in (≈ 10 %) and lowering ≈ 8° during the rise. Ghost of the flat drawing remains on the page (≈ 30 %) until 2.4.
- build: each object is a set of 3D polylines (edges of simple primitives); `pos.z_i = height_i·ease(u_i)` and for "fold-up" parts rotate about their base edge from 90° to 0°; render with three.js `LineSegments` (+ `LineMaterial` for constant width) or project to SVG paths each frame. Props: `objects[]` (`{type: box|cylinder|path, size, pos, rotateFromFlat}`), `stagger`, `riseFrames`, `inkColor`, `lineWidth`, `pageGhost`.
- source: x3 1.60–2.60 s (`t_popup.png`).
- value: turning a diagram, floor plan or illustration into a 3D model; "from idea on paper to real thing" stories.

### `sketch-shade-in` — NEW (relates to `split-compare` line-art vs colour)
- look: once the ink-line model is complete, shading arrives: surfaces fill with white clay, cross-hatching appears on the shadow sides (dense parallel pen strokes following the surface direction), and soft cast shadows from the window light fade in on the page, all within a few tenths of a second; the sun icon above the rack (a line circle with rays) appears just before, as if it switched the light on.
- params: sun icon 2.45–2.60 (circle Ø ≈ 0.045 W draws on, 12 rays pop out, 1.5 px ink); fill + hatch 2.70–3.10 (easeInOutQuad opacity, hatch spacing ≈ 5 px at 1280 w, 1 px strokes at 70 % ink, angle per face); cast shadows 2.80–3.20 (`#000` at ≈ 25 %, long, toward the lower right, matching the desk's window light).
- build: three.js toon pipeline: `MeshToonMaterial` white + an edge pass (`EdgesGeometry` or Sobel on normals/depth) + a screen-space hatching shader (`hatch = step(fract(dot(uv, dir)·freq), 1 − darkness)` with darkness from N·L), crossfading `shadeMix` 0 → 1. SVG fallback: per-face fills with `<pattern>` hatches whose opacity follows `shadeMix`. Props: `shadeFrames`, `hatchSpacing`, `hatchAngle`, `lightDir`, `shadowOpacity`, `sunIcon`.
- source: x3 2.45–3.20 s (`t_popup.png`, `t_fill.png`).
- value: the "render pass" reveal: sketch → finished look; architecture/product visualisation; an NPR finish for any three.js motion.

### `energy-jar-fill` — NEW
- look: a glass jar (ink-line cylinder) on a shelf lights up: coloured, self-lit liquid rises from the bottom with a soft meniscus, and its glow spills onto the shelf, the jar's label card and the neighbouring jars as a coloured tint; the label's handwritten value counts up. Draining is the reverse with the glow fading. Several jars can be lit in different colours; the shelf back panel tints toward the active colour.
- params: fill 0.35–0.45 s (3.45→3.80 purple; 4.20→4.60 blue), easeOutCubic on level; level 0 → 70–80 % of jar height; liquid colour fully saturated (`#7E2AE4`, `#2A79EB`, ≈ `#F2741E`, `#2DFF71`, pink `#E86AA8`), inner gradient lighter at top; glow = additive radial (radius ≈ 1.5 jar widths, colour at 35–50 %) plus a tint on the shelf plank below (≈ 30 %); drain 0.3 s easeInCubic. Label: small card under each jar, store name (serif 7 px) + handwritten value; counter interpolates in 0.3 s with 2 significant figures.
- build: SVG: jar = ellipse rim + two lines + ellipse base (ink); liquid = `<clipPath>` of the jar interior holding a rect whose top `y = base − level·h` with a meniscus ellipse; glow = `<radialGradient>` circle with `mix-blend-mode: screen`; tint = coloured rect under the shelf at low opacity. three.js: emissive cylinder + `PointLight` in the jar colour (intensity ∝ level). Props: `jars[]` (`{label, color, value}`), `timeline` (`[{jar, level, at}]`), `glow`, `labels`, `valueFormat`.
- source: x3 3.40–4.60, 5.60–6.20, 11.6–13.5, 22–28 s (`t_fill.png`, `t_cut.png`, `b_e.png`).
- value: visualising stores/quantities (energy, budget, storage, progress); a glowing, more physical alternative to bar charts.

### `transfer-arc` — NEW (relates to `map-route`)
- look: when energy moves from one jar to another, a thick arched tube draws itself from the top of the source jar over to the target jar; its colour is a gradient from the source colour to the target colour, and the target jar fills as the arc completes while the source level drops a little. Chains of arcs (purple → blue → orange → pink) show multi-step transfers.
- params: arc draw 3.90→4.20 s (≈ 0.3 s, easeInOutCubic, stroke ≈ 5 px at 1280 w, round caps), arc height ≈ 0.6 × the jar spacing above the rims; gradient purple `#7E2AE4` → `#A891D7` → blue `#2A79EB`; target fill starts when the arc reaches 80 %; source level −10 %; arcs persist (soft glow) until the state resets.
- build: SVG `<path d="M a Q mid b">` with `pathLength=1` and `strokeDasharray` reveal; `<linearGradient gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse">` between the two jar positions; optional flowing dashes along the arc after it is drawn (dashoffset loop). Props: `from`, `to`, `colorFrom`, `colorTo`, `height`, `drawFrames`, `flow`.
- source: x3 3.90–4.60, 11.6–13.3 s (`t_fill.png`, `t_card.png`).
- value: showing flows/transfers between containers (energy, money, data between services).

### `cutaway-glow-core` — EXTENDS `xray-scan` (new 3D `cutaway` mode)
- look: a hatched ink-and-clay battery is shown in half-section: the outer can is cut away along its length (hatched cut faces), revealing a saturated glowing green chemical core inside; the core's glow tints the floor. The camera drifts slowly around it.
- params: core `#2DFF71` with a slightly lighter centre, emissive (bloom ≈ 6 px), can wall ≈ 12 % of the radius, cut faces hatched at 45° every ≈ 6 px; camera arc ≈ 8° over 2 s, easeInOutSine.
- build: three.js: `CylinderGeometry(thetaStart, thetaLength = π)` for the can halves + cap planes with a hatch texture; emissive inner cylinder; SVG fallback: 2D section drawing (rect with hatched walls and a glowing rounded core). Props: `object` (`battery | pipe | cell | custom`), `coreColor`, `cutAngle`, `orbitDeg`.
- source: x3 7.55–9.60 s (`f_8.5.png`, `t_move.png`).
- value: "what's inside" explanations for products and science.

### `live-quantity-labels` — EXTENDS `number-counter` (new `attachTo` 3D label cards + `handwritten` face)
- look: small paper label cards stuck to objects show a quantity in handwritten digits; during an event (the book falling) the numbers update live: one drops (15 J → 14 J), another grows rapidly (0.00011 J → 0.01 → 0.1 → 0.21 → 0.56 → 0.98 J).
- params: update every ≈ 0.1 s (not every frame), value follows the physics (kinetic ∝ t² during the fall: `E = ½ m (g t)²`); significant figures adapt (0.00011, 0.01, 0.1, 0.21); the card is a cream `#F2EEE4` rectangle with an ink border and a tiny serif name.
- build: label = SVG group anchored to a projected 3D point; `value(t)` from a user function or keyframes; formatter `sig(2)`; font Caveat. Props: `attachTo`, `value` (fn or keyframes), `unit`, `stepFrames`, `format`.
- source: x3 9.63–11.60 s (`t_fall.png`).
- value: live measurements in physics/finance explainers.

### `glass-lesson-card` — NEW (relates to `scene-tag`, `story-caption-box`)
- look: a frosted-glass card floats top-left over the 3D scene: small letter-spaced red-brown eyebrow ("LESSON 1", "THE EQUATION", "KEY WORDS", "PREDICT", "WHY?"), a serif heading, and 1–4 short lines of body (or a definition list, or a checklist). It blurs whatever is behind it; the top is warm cream and fades to a translucent grey-green at the bottom where the scene shows through. Cards replace each other with a short crossfade + 8 px upward drift.
- params: ≈ 0.34 W × 0.26 H; radius 14 px; fill `#F5EDE0` at 85 % → `#CBD2CB` at 55 % (vertical), `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(1.1)`; 1 px inner highlight `#FFFFFF` at 40 %; soft shadow 0 8 24 `#000` 12 %. Eyebrow 9 px caps, tracking +12 %, `#683836`; heading 20 px semibold `#22211A`; body 15 px `#3B3B38`. Swap: old card fades 0.15 s, new fades in 0.25 s with y +8 → 0 (easeOutCubic). Each card holds 0.6–1.5 s here (driven by clicks).
- build: HTML/CSS in Remotion (`backdropFilter` works in Chrome headless) or SVG fallback with a blurred copy of the scene clipped to the card. Props: `cards[]` (`{eyebrow, heading, body | items[] | checklist[]}`), `position`, `holdFrames`, `width`, `theme` (`paper` | `dark`).
- source: x3 13.9–22.0, 25.4–26.0 s (`b_d.png`, `f_14.5.png`).
- value: lesson / step / definition overlays for explainers; the card system of an educational product video.

### `magnifier-loupe` — NEW (fx, overlay-capable)
- look: a round lens follows the cursor across the page text, magnifying it ≈ 1.6× with a slight barrel bulge; its rim has a thin prismatic (chromatic) fringe in orange/magenta and a soft white edge highlight; a small magnifier icon sits at its centre.
- params: lens Ø ≈ 0.12 W (150 px at 1280 w); zoom 1.6×; rim fringe ≈ 3 px orange `#F6BC7C` outside, magenta `#E38AE8` inside; white rim 1 px at 70 %; drop shadow 0 4 10 `#000` 15 %. It eases toward the cursor (≈ 0.1 s lag, critically damped).
- build: SVG: `<clipPath>` circle around a copy of the target layer scaled about the lens centre; barrel effect via `feDisplacementMap` with a radial map (optional); fringe = two offset rings with `mix-blend-mode: screen`. Path = keyframed points or a seeded "reading" wander. Props: `target` (layer id), `path` (keyframes), `zoom`, `size`, `fringe`.
- source: x3 13.9–20.0 s (`f_14.5.png`, `b_d.png`).
- value: drawing attention to fine print, a UI detail, a chart value; "look closer" beats.

### `lift-the-flap` — NEW
- look: a cream paper flap printed with "Lift the flap to see the answer" (with a hand-drawn arrow "lift here") lifts from its bottom edge, flips up and over toward the viewer, showing its blank underside, and folds away, revealing the answer paragraph printed underneath; a small tab remains.
- params: lift 19.60–19.75 s (≈ 0.15 s, easeInOutCubic) rotating 0 → ≈ 160° about its top edge (rotateX), with a growing shadow beneath; underside slightly darker paper; answer text already printed under it.
- build: CSS 3D / SVG `scaleY = cos θ` with a skew for perspective; underside drawn when `cos θ < 0`; shadow rect opacity ∝ sin θ. Props: `frontText`, `answerText`, `openAt`, `frames`, `hinge` (`top` | `left`), `paperColor`.
- source: x3 19.55–19.80 s (`t_flap.png`).
- value: quiz/answer reveals, "before you guess…" moments, interactive-feeling explainers.

### `player-toolbar` — NEW (ui, overlay-capable)
- look: a frosted pill toolbar centred at the bottom like a media/lesson player: menu, back, play/pause, a serif caption describing the current step (wraps to two lines), a 5-dot pager with the current dot as a dash, next, a divider, mute, contrast and course icons. The caption text swaps per step with a quick fade.
- params: see Overview > UI layout; glass `#C7CEC7` at 80 % with blur 16 px; icons 1.5 px stroke `#2B2B2B`; caption 17 px serif; pager dot 5 px, active dash 16×5 px; caption swap 0.2 s.
- build: HTML/CSS in Remotion; `steps: [{at, caption, page}]`. Props: `steps`, `icons`, `theme`, `width`.
- source: x3 whole video.
- value: makes a narrated explainer look like a real product; doubles as an on-screen caption bar.

## Camera

Real 3D perspective camera, ≈ 35–40° FOV. Opening: high 3/4 view (≈ 50° elevation) of the closed book, pushing in and lowering during the open. On the pop-up: low 3/4 close on the rack (≈ 30° elevation). Close-ups are reached by **snap dollies** (≈ 0.3 s) followed by slow drifts (≈ 2–4 % zoom per second, small orbit ≈ 4°/s). One hard cut to a low side-on angle for the fall. Lesson section: locked wide on the open spread. A soft window-light gobo (diagonal light band with mullion shadow) lies across the desk and stays fixed in world space, which sells the depth when the camera moves.

## Styles

### style `ink-hatch-3d` — Ink-hatched clay 3D (pop-up textbook)
- essence: white clay 3D objects drawn with black pen contours and cross-hatching, sitting on real paper in a photoreal desk world; colour is reserved for a few glowing, saturated "energy" liquids; frosted glass UI with book-serif type.
- palette: bg (desk) `#BEB3A2`, mat `#8FA194`, paper `#EFEDE9`, ink `#1E1E1E`, clay `#F4F2EE`, hatch `#1E1E1E` at 60–70 %, accent purple `#7E2AE4`, accent2 blue `#2A79EB`, accent3 orange ≈ `#F2741E`, accent4 green `#2DFF71`, UI glass `#F5EDE0` / `#C7CEC7`, eyebrow red `#683836`, book cloth `#D6A73C`.
- texture / post: NPR on the content: contour lines (constant 1.5 px) + screen-space hatching driven by lighting (two directions, second only below 40 % light); soft shadows; additive bloom only on emissive liquids (radius ≈ 0.5 % W); world: slight DOF on far desk, warm window gobo. Pixel-style engine approximation (for flat motions): `edges` ink 1.5 px → `hatch` (luma < 0.6, 45°, 5 px) → keep saturated hues above 0.7 saturation, desaturate the rest → paper overlay `#EFEDE9` 6 %.
- typography: **Source Serif 4** (or Literata/Newsreader) for headings/body/captions/toolbar; small-caps letter-spaced eyebrows in the same serif; **Caveat** for handwritten values and margin notes; italic serif for figure captions and equations.
- motion rules: 60 fps; physical page/cover motion with easeInOutCubic; pop-ups with small easeOutBack overshoot, staggered back-to-front; snap dollies (0.3 s) then slow drifts; colour enters only as light (fill + glow), never as flat paint; UI elements crossfade with an 8 px drift.
- signature moves: `book-open-3d`, `lineart-popup-extrude`, `sketch-shade-in`, `energy-jar-fill`, `transfer-arc`, `cutaway-glow-core`, `live-quantity-labels`, `glass-lesson-card`, `magnifier-loupe`, `lift-the-flap`, `player-toolbar`.
- do: keep the content monochrome ink + clay and let one or two glowing colours carry the meaning; label everything with small handwritten values; keep the UI quiet and serif. don't: colour the objects themselves, use sans-serif UI, add outlines to the world layer (desk/book cover stay photoreal).
- source: x3 (screen capture by someone else; analysis only).
