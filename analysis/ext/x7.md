## Motion techniques

### `vs-splash` — NEW
- look: a fighting-game versus card: the frame splits along a steep diagonal into a blue-striped left panel and a red-striped right panel, each with a fighter portrait and a big italic name; a round dark VS badge with a gold crescent pops in the centre with overshoot, a calligraphic "对决" pops above, small mascot icons wobble below; then "FIGHT!" slams in over everything with radial speed lines and the card darkens.
- params: 1 neutral grey frame (12.47) as an impact beat; panels slide in from their sides 12.55→12.72 (≈ 4 f, easeOutCubic) closing a black diagonal gap with a white 0.4 % W divider (diagonal ≈ 70° from horizontal, top at 0.58 W, bottom at 0.41 W). Stripes 1.5 % W wide at the same angle, 2 tones per panel (blue `#152FA2`/`#2349CC`, red `#D21851`/`#AE1549`), plus speckle grain. VS badge: scale 0.45 (12.80) → 1.12 (12.88) → 1.0 (12.97) (spring, damping ≈ 10), Ø 0.27 W. "对决" pop 13.05→13.13 with the same spring. Names at y 0.86 H, cap height 6 % H. Hold ≈ 1.2 s, slight continuous push 1 %/s. "FIGHT!" at 14.22: scale 1.4 → 1.0 in 3 f, rotation −6°, panels dim 40 %, 40–60 radial speed lines (white 30 %). Cut 14.55.
- build: SVG: two `<polygon>` panels with a `<pattern>` of skewed stripes; `translateX` per panel; badge = circle + crescent path + text with `paint-order: stroke` and an offset dark copy for the extrude; `spring()` for pops; reuse `speed-lines` for the FIGHT! burst. Props: `left {name, src, color}`, `right {…}`, `badgeText='VS'`, `topText`, `slamText='FIGHT!'`, `diagonalDeg`, `slamAt`, `font`.
- source: x7 12.47–14.55 s (`b_12.3.png`, `f_13.5.png`).
- value: any A-vs-B comparison (products, models, plans, teams); sports and esports promos.

### `beam-clash` — NEW

### `token-pipeline` — NEW
- look: a Rube-Goldberg lab: small glowing cubes stream one by one through glass tubes, around a gold-ringed glass coil, and out of a dropper that swells a drop until it falls into a labelled flask — "data/tokens go in, a product comes out". The camera follows the flow from vessel to vessel.
- params: cube size 0.8–1.2 % W, orange `#F27A2E` with a bright face, 0.07 s spacing, speed ≈ 0.5 W/s along the tube path, slight tumble (90°/0.3 s); coil = 3–4 turns, tubes white-highlighted glass (2 px white spec line + 25 % tint). Drop: swells 0→1.6 % W over 0.5 s (easeOutQuad), glint flash 1 frame, falls with gravity (≈ 0.33 s for 0.35 H). Camera pans/tilts with easeInOutSine between vessels, ≈ 1.2 s per stage.
- build: SVG path for the tube network; cubes placed with `getPointAtLength((t - i·gap)·speed)`; glass via gradient strokes + spec highlight; drop as a `<path>` that pinches off (scaleY 1.3 at release). Camera = group translate following the lead cube with lag. Props: `path` (or preset `lab`), `cubeColor`, `count`, `gap`, `speed`, `labels {source, target}`, `outputColor`.
- source: x7 0.0–3.75 s (`b_0.png`).
- value: training / build / pipeline explainers ("your data goes in…"), product origin stories.

## Styles

### style `arcade-halftone` — Spider-Verse arcade
- essence: chunky toon 3D characters printed like a comic: halftone dots in the shadows, heavy spray-grain speckle, chromatic rims and neon reflections, under fighting-game UI typography.
- palette: bg `#100929`, bg2 `#1C2154`, ink `#03011B`, paper/text `#FCFAE3`, accent (player 1 red) `#E66154`, accent2 (player 2 blue) `#3598F6`, neon pink `#E54B8E`, neon cyan `#55C5F9`, gold `#F8CB61`.
- texture / post: (1) halftone in shadows — luminance-masked dot pattern: `halftone` step (size 5–6 px at 1080p, angle 45°, ink = darker shade of the local colour, `mix-blend: multiply`, mask = 1 − luminance > 0.45); (2) spray grain — per-frame seeded speckle (1–2 px dots, 4–8 % coverage, white and colour-tinted, `screen` blend on darks) — `grain` step amount 0.12 size 1 with colour; (3) chromatic rims — `misregister dx 4 dy 0 color #E54B8E opacity 0.7` + mirrored cyan; (4) radial focus-line rays behind the subject (thin, 30 % opacity, gold and cyan); (5) slight bloom on neon (`glow radius 8 amount 0.4`); (6) checkerboard floor with mirrored reflections at 35 %.
- typography: **Bangers** (exclamations), **Lilita One** skewed −8° (names, banners), **Rajdhani** Bold tracked (sub-labels), **Zhi Mang Xing** (CJK accent). All headline text: cream fill, 3–6 px dark extrude down-right, 2 px dark outline.
- motion rules: 24 fps; snappy springs (pops in 3–5 frames with ≈ 10 % overshoot); squash & stretch on every contact; 1-frame tinted flash on every cut; holds of 1.2–2.5 s; UI bars enter in 1–2 frames then drift; camera shakes 3 f on hits.
- signature moves: `vs-splash`, `fighter-nameplate`, `fight-hud`, `super-move-splash`, `beam-clash`, `slant-banner`, `slime-hop`, `rocket-to-moon`, `comic-burst`, `speed-lines`, `sign-drop`.
- do: two strong opposing colours (one per side), keep text in the extruded italic style, grain on everything. don't: use thin fonts for headlines, soft slow fades, or more than two hero colours plus gold.
- source: x7 (someone else's promo; analysis only).
