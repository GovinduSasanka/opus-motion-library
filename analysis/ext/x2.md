## Motion techniques

### `timeline-bounce-study` — EXTENDS `bouncing-ball` (new `timeline`, `trail: 'dots'`, `labels` props)
- look: a red ball bounces along a thin horizontal axis like a timing chart: every contact stamps a tick with a time label under the axis, the ball's past positions stay as small grey dots (a spacing chart), and a leader-line label states the squash/stretch factors.
- params (30 fps tracking, 1080p): bounce period **0.50 s** exactly (contacts at 0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 s); apex at mid-period, apex height **≈ 0.31 H** (centre y 408 → contact ≈ 740); horizontal speed constant ≈ 510 px/s (≈ 0.27 W/s); ball Ø ≈ 120 px (0.11 H). In flight near contact: stretch to h/w ≈ 1.38 (144×104 px) along the velocity; at apex round (124×112 slightly wide); contact frame squash ≈ ×0.64 high / ×1.5 wide for 1–2 frames on the final landing. Axis: 2 px `#1E181E`, from 0.13 W to 0.87 W, draws on in ≈ 0.15 s. Trail dots: Ø 4 px, grey `#9A9690`, one per 2 frames. Tick labels: mono 12 px under the axis at each contact.
- build: `y = contactY − 4·apex·u·(1−u)` with `u = (t mod T)/T`; `x = x0 + v·t`; stretch `s = 1 + k·|vy|/vmax` along the velocity angle; on contact frames `scaleY 0.64, scaleX 1.5`. Store positions every 2 frames for the dot trail. Props: `period`, `apex`, `speed`, `bounces`, `stretch`, `squash`, `timeline: {ticks, labelFormat}`, `trail`, `labels` (callout text), `color`.
- source: x2 0.0–1.7 s (`b_0.png`, `ball/`).
- value: teaching timing / physics; a "principles" explainer; product of the "made in code" genre.

### `ball-zoom-flood` — NEW (transition)
- look: the bouncing ball leaves its path, flies toward the camera and grows until its flat colour floods the whole frame, which becomes the next chapter's background (same red-orange).
- params: 1.70→1.95 s: Ø 112 px (1.70) → 244 (1.80) → 448 (1.87) → full frame ≈ 1.95; growth is exponential (easeInExpo); the ball moves from (1300, 630) toward frame centre while growing; heavy motion blur on its edge (soft 30–60 px feather) in the last 3 frames. The next chapter's HUD is already there underneath.
- build: transition variant of `zoom`/`iris`: a circle `r = r0·e^{k·u}` at a path point lerped to the centre, filled with `toColor`, with `feGaussianBlur` ∝ dr/dt on its edge; when `r > hypot(W,H)/2` swap scenes. Props: `from` (point or subject id), `color`, `frames` (default 15 @60), `blur`.
- source: x2 1.68–1.97 s (`t_ball.png`, `b_0.png`).
- value: chapter change that grows from the hero object; "zoom into the idea".

### `bezier-handle-graph` — EXTENDS `easing-graph` (new `handles`, `lanes`, `liveCaption` props)
- look: a progress-vs-time graph builds (axes draw on, a straight linear line), then two handles slide out and bend the line into a steep S curve; the caption below prints the live `cubic-bezier(x1, y1, x2, y2)` values as they change. On the right, four labelled lanes (LINEAR, EASE-OUT, CUBIC-BEZIER, SPRING) each run a dot from left to right with per-frame tick marks behind it, so spacing shows the easing; the spring lane overshoots and wobbles at the end.
- params: axes 1.95–2.15 s, linear line 2.15–2.30, handles pull 2.30–2.50 (end values (0.92, −0.06, 0.08, 1.06) then settling to (0.85, −0.00, 0.15, 1.00) by 2.9 s); graph box ≈ 0.27 W × 0.48 H at left (x 0.04–0.31 W); lanes at x 0.38–0.72 W, 4 rows ≈ 0.055 H apart; race 2.7–3.5 s; lane dots Ø 18 px black, the bezier lane dot white with black ring. Lines 2 px black on red-orange; grid 1 px at 25 % black.
- build: SVG path `M0,0 C x1,y1 x2,y2 1,1` scaled to the box; handles = lines + dots at the control points, animated with `interpolate`; caption string from rounded values; lanes = `easing-graph` tracks with `ease: linear | out | bezier(x1..) | spring({damping:10})`, ticks every 2 frames. Props: `from` / `to` bezier arrays, `lanes[]`, `bg`, `ink`.
- source: x2 1.95–3.58 s (`b_0.png`, `b_1.png`, `sheet.png`).
- value: explaining easing / API docs for animation libraries; developer-tool marketing.

### `stepped-diagonal-wipe` — NEW (transition)
- look: a broad lime diagonal band with a **pixel-stepped (staircase) edge** sweeps from the right edge to the left at about −30°, carrying the next colour (ultramarine) behind it; the band is ≈ 0.25 W wide and both its edges are hard 8-step staircases, as if drawn at low resolution.
- params: 3.58→3.92 s (20 frames @60, easeInOutCubic); band angle ≈ 30° from vertical (top leads); step size ≈ 0.045 H vertical × 0.03 W horizontal; colours lime `#D2FC36` band, new bg behind. A faint 1-frame horizontal smear on the band's leading edge.
- build: like the existing `diagonal` transition but the mask polygon is quantised: compute the edge line `x(y)` and snap `y` to `steps` rows, building a staircase polygon; two masks (band lead edge, band trail edge). Props: `angle`, `steps`, `bandWidth`, `bandColor`, `frames`. Could be a `variant: 'stepped'` of `diagonal`.
- source: x2 3.55–3.95 s (`t_stair.png`, `b_1.png`).
- value: punchy colour-block chapter change for tech/retro-digital looks.

### `shape-morph-chain` — NEW
- look: one flat shape morphs through a chain of forms inside a design-tool selection box (thin box with 8 square handles and a size tag "401 × 401" under it, plus faint concentric guide circles): lime **circle** → cream **squircle** (rotated 45°) → red **triangle** → mustard → lime **star** → cream **flower** (8 scallops) → small red **dot**. Each morph also hops colour and spins a little, with a short overshoot, and the caption under it reads "CIRCLE → SQUIRCLE", "SQUIRCLE → TRIANGLE", "TRIANGLE → STAR", "STAR → FLOWER".
- params: entry 3.92–4.17: dot Ø 20 px → circle overshoot Ø 0.43 H at 4.17 → settles Ø 0.37 H (≈ 401 px) by 4.4 (spring, ≈ 15 % overshoot). Each morph ≈ 0.25–0.3 s with a 0.15–0.25 s hold: squircle 4.5–4.8 (+45° rotation), triangle 4.9–5.15, star 5.25–5.4 (+36° spin), flower 5.5–5.6, shrink to red dot Ø 0.07 H 5.6–5.75. Selection box tracks the shape's bounds with a 2-frame lag; handles 6 px squares, box stroke 1 px white at 70 %; size tag lime pill, mono 10 px. Colour changes are cross-fades over the morph (lime `#D2FC36` → cream `#EADED8` → salmon → red `#FC491D` → mustard → lime → cream → red).
- build: resample every shape to the same number of points (e.g. 120) starting at the top and walking clockwise, then `lerp` point by point with an easeInOutBack(1.2) curve (`flubber`-style interpolation is fine, but point-resampling keeps it dependency-free and deterministic); rotation and fill interpolate in parallel. Shapes defined parametrically: superellipse (n = 4) for the squircle, polygons with rounded joins (`stroke-linejoin round` + same-colour stroke) for triangle/star, `r = R(1 + 0.12 cos 8θ)` for the flower. Props: `shapes[]` (`circle | squircle | triangle | star | flower | polygon:n | path`), `colors[]`, `holdFrames`, `morphFrames`, `showBox`, `captions`, `bg`.
- source: x2 3.92–5.75 s (`b_1.png`, `b_2.png`).
- value: "form / transformation / flexibility" beats; logo reveals that morph from a primitive; design-tool marketing.

### `line-open-wipe` — NEW (transition)
- look: the shape becomes a small red dot; a thin white horizontal line shoots out from the dot to both frame edges, then the line opens vertically into a band of the next chapter's paper colour that grows to fill the frame (with vertical motion blur on its edges). The dot stays and becomes the dot in "M●TION".
- params: line draw 5.75→5.80 (3 f, easeOutExpo, 3 px white); band open 5.80→5.95 (9 f, easeInOutCubic) from 3 px to full height, edges feathered ≈ 20 px while moving. Dot stays at frame centre-ish (0.5 W, 0.5 H) and then jumps to its place in the word.
- build: mask = rect of height `h(u)` centred at the dot's y; pre-phase draws the line `scaleX 0→1` from the dot. Props: `center`, `lineColor`, `frames`, `lineFrames`. Relates to `bands:center` but with the line pre-phase and a held anchor object.
- source: x2 5.70–5.97 s (`t_line.png`, `b_2.png`).
- value: elegant "open the shutter" chapter change around an anchor object.

### `type-marquee-field` — NEW
- look: a bold word ("MOTION", with the O replaced by the red dot) rises into place letter by letter from a baseline mask; then rows of the same word fill the frame above and below it, alternating outline (2 px stroke) and solid black rows, each scrolling sideways in opposite directions; the whole field rolls to about −8° and pushes in, so the word becomes a texture.
- params: letter rise 5.95–6.25 (stagger ≈ 1.5 f/letter, each 8 f easeOutCubic from +0.6 cap height, clipped at the baseline); centre word cap height ≈ 0.13 H, tracking −2 %; rows fade in 6.4 (outline rows first); row pitch ≈ 0.17 H; marquee speed ≈ 0.15 W/s, alternating ±; roll 0 → −8° and scale 1 → 1.12 over 6.5–7.0 (easeInOutQuad). Dot = red circle with Ø = cap height.
- build: `mask-rise-title` for the hero word; rows = `<text>` repeated with `x = ((t·v + rowOffset) mod wordWidth) − wordWidth`, `fill` none + `stroke` for outline rows; wrap all in `<g transform="rotate(r) scale(s)">`. Props: `word`, `dotLetter`, `rowCount`, `outlineEvery`, `speed`, `roll`, `push`, `font`.
- source: x2 5.95–7.05 s (`b_3.png`, `sheet.png`).
- value: kinetic type backgrounds for brand words; poster-style title cards. (The rise itself is COVERED by `mask-rise-title`; the marquee field + roll is new.)

### `whip-blur-cut` — COVERED by transition `push-blur`
- look: the type field whips sideways with heavy horizontal blur (≈ 4 frames), cut to black on the blurred frame.
- params: 7.05–7.20 s; blur ramps 0 → ≈ 120 px horizontal; motion ≈ 0.4 W in 4 frames (easeInQuad), hard cut to the next chapter at peak blur.
- source: x2 7.0–7.2 s (`t_whip.png`).

### `slice-assemble-glyph` — NEW
- look: on black, a big glyph pair ("动效") is cut into ≈ 8–10 horizontal slices of different heights; the slices fly in from left and right at different speeds (some overshoot) and lock together into the finished glyphs; a few slices briefly sit misaligned like a digital glitch. Under it the mono caption decodes from random characters to "MOTION DESIGN"; then the red dot pops on at the glyph's top-right.
- params: 7.20–7.50 s (≈ 18 f): slice heights 4–14 % of glyph height; start offsets ±0.3–0.6 W; each slice easeOutExpo over 6–12 f, random start in the first 6 f; final 2 f snap. Glyph height ≈ 0.28 H, cream `#EADED8` on `#0C0B10`. Caption decode 7.30–7.55 (≈ 2 random chars per frame). Dot pop 7.58 (Ø 0 → 22 → 18 px, 4 f).
- build: render the text once into a `<clipPath>` per slice (`<rect y=.. height=..>`), offset each slice group by `dx_i·(1 − easeOutExpo(u_i))`; seeded heights/offsets. Props: `text`, `slices`, `spread`, `frames`, `font`, `color`, `caption` (uses `glyph-orbit-logo`'s scramble for the decode).
- source: x2 7.20–7.60 s (`t_whip.png`, `b_3.png`).
- value: punchy logo / word reveal with a glitch flavour but clean result; great for CJK and display type.

### `particle-shape-morph` — NEW (relates to `pointcloud-orbit`)
- look: a title glyph dissolves into thousands of sand-like coloured particles (cream with a few red and lime specks) which sweep, left glyph first, into a slowly rotating **sphere** centred on the red dot; the sphere then flattens and opens into a tilted **torus**, spins, and finally **collapses** into a tiny glowing red-orange core with a bloom. A mono caption tracks it: "N = 5,150 · GLYPH → SPHERE", "N = 6,218 · SPHERE → TORUS", "COLLAPSE".
- params: N ≈ 5–6 k points, Ø 2–4 px, colours cream `#E8E2D8` (≈ 85 %), red `#FC491D` (≈ 8 %), lime `#D2FC36` (≈ 7 %). Glyph → particles 7.75–8.0 (texture breaks up); glyph → sphere 8.25–8.9 (per-particle delay by x position, ≈ 0.4 s spread, easeInOutCubic, slight swirl); sphere radius ≈ 0.31 H, Fibonacci-lattice points, rotating ≈ 30°/s about a tilted y-axis; sphere → torus 9.25–9.5 (R ≈ 0.32 H, r ≈ 0.12 H, viewed ≈ 60° from the axis); collapse 9.8→9.92 (easeInCubic) to Ø ≈ 0.08 H with orange bloom radius ≈ 0.25 H. Core dot always drawn on top, with a soft red glow.
- build: three.js `Points` (or canvas 2D with projected points). Targets: sample the glyph (render text to an offscreen canvas, pick N pixels), Fibonacci sphere, torus (u,v grid). Per particle `p = lerp(A_i, B_i, ease(clamp((t − t0 − d_i)/dur)))` with `d_i` from x order; rotate the whole set; depth-sort and size by depth. Props: `text`, `count`, `shapes` (`glyph | sphere | torus | ring | cube | image`), `colors`, `stageFrames`, `rotation`, `captions`, `collapse`.
- source: x2 7.75–9.95 s (`b_4.png`, `f_8.9.png`).
- value: "data / ideas take shape" moments; logo-to-particle intros; tech product hero loops.

### `core-shockwave-reveal` — NEW (transition)
- look: the collapsed glowing core flashes; the frame goes flat grey for a frame, then a disc of the next chapter's colour (blue) expands from the core with a soft **lime/yellow chromatic fringe ring** on its edge (like a shockwave), while white particles spray radially as short streaks inside it; the streaks slow down and become the next chapter's dash grid.
- params: flash 9.92–9.97 (white-orange bloom); grey `#5A5A5A` 1 frame; disc 9.97→10.20 from r ≈ 0.25 H to past the corners (easeOutCubic), fringe width ≈ 0.04 H lime `#C8D86A` blending to grey outside; a second fainter ring trails 0.1 s behind; streak spray 10.0–10.25, streak length 10–40 px, decelerating; centre keeps a dark hole Ø ≈ 0.1 H that closes by 10.3.
- build: transition with `center`; draw new scene clipped to circle r(u); fringe = two stacked circles with `stroke` lime and `feGaussianBlur`; streaks = N radial line segments with length ∝ speed. Props: `center`, `fringeColor`, `frames`, `streaks`.
- source: x2 9.80–10.30 s (`t_ring.png`, `b_4.png`, `b_5.png`).
- value: big energetic "release" after a build-up; product-launch reveals.

### `dash-flow-field` — NEW (relates to `flow-lines`)
- look: a regular grid of short rounded white dashes on ultramarine. Each dash rotates to follow a vector field around a red dot that wanders across the frame: first the dashes circle it (vortex), then point at it (radial), then circle again. A wave of **lime, longer** dashes travels outward from the dot like a ripple ring, and the dashes near the dot shrink to dots (a calm eye).
- params: grid pitch 80 px (0.042 W) in both axes → 24×14 dashes; dash 32 px long, 10 px thick, round caps, cream `#E8E6DC`; lime `#D2FC36` dashes grow to 56 px. Ripple: lime band ≈ 1.5 cells wide travelling ≈ 0.45 W/s, one every ≈ 0.6 s. Dot Ø 50 px `#F6481E`, wanders on a slow Lissajous (≈ 0.3 W/s). Field mode switches 10.9 (vortex → radial, 4-frame blend) and 11.1 (radial → vortex). Dashes within ≈ 1 cell of the dot scale to length 0 (dots).
- build: for each grid point `p`: `d = p − dot`, `θ = atan2(d) + (mode === 'vortex' ? π/2 : 0)`, blend modes with `mix`; `len = base·smoothstep(0, 1.5 cell, |d|)`; ripple `k = exp(−((|d| − v·(t − t0))/w)²)` → `len += 24·k`, colour mix to lime by `k`. Pure SVG `<line>`s with `stroke-linecap round`. Props: `pitch`, `dashLength`, `thickness`, `mode` keyframes, `attractor` path, `rippleEvery`, `colors`, `bg`.
- source: x2 10.25–11.55 s (`b_5.png`, `f_10.9.png`, `t_blind.png`).
- value: ambient "rhythm / data flow / magnetic field" backgrounds; interactive-feeling hero loops (the attractor can be the cursor on the web).

### `field-blinds-wipe` — NEW (transition)
- look: the dashes of the flow field fatten and rotate into overlapping rectangular tiles, then snap horizontal and become venetian-blind slats that open onto the next colour (paper), the slats shrinking to thin lines that vanish.
- params: 11.55–11.75 s: dash thickness 10 → 40 px (6 f), rotation to 0° (4 f), slat height 80 px → 0 (6 f, easeInCubic) revealing paper `#EDE9E1`.
- build: reuse the field's elements; tween `thickness`, `angle → 0`, then a slat mask; generic version = `bands` transition with `variant: 'blinds'` driven by the outgoing scene's grid. Props: `pitch`, `frames`, `angleSnap`.
- source: x2 11.50–11.80 s (`t_blind.png`, `b_5.png`).
- value: transition that grows out of a pattern background instead of over it.

### `breathing-end-card` — EXTENDS `mask-rise-title` (new `breatheDot`, `eyebrowType`, `rule` props)
- look: the red dot drops in from above (motion-blurred), lands to the right of where the headline will be with a squash and one ring; the headline "让设计呼吸" rises in glyph by glyph; a thin rule draws under it from the centre out with a small red tick; a mono eyebrow above types "MOTION DESIGN REEL — 15 SECONDS" behind a red block cursor; the English line "Motion makes design breathe." fades in under the rule. Then the dot "breathes": it swells and shrinks once per second and emits a thin red ring each breath.
- params: dot drop 11.80→12.00 (easeInQuad, vertical blur), landing squash 2 f; glyph rise 12.05–12.30 (stagger ≈ 2.5 f/glyph); rule 12.25–12.45 (≈ 0.40 W wide, 1.5 px, `#1E181E`); eyebrow typing 12.30–12.90 at ≈ 1 char / 1.2 f, cursor 8×12 px red, blink 2 Hz; subtitle fade 12.45–12.70. Breath: dot Ø 26 → 34 → 26 px, period ≈ 1.0 s (easeInOutSine); ring per breath r 20 → 80 px, 1.5 px stroke, opacity 0.8 → 0. Headline: cap height ≈ 0.10 H, centred at 0.5 W, y ≈ 0.45 H.
- build: compose `mask-rise-title` + `typewriter-signature` (eyebrow, with cursor) + `sonar-ping`-style rings on the dot. Props: `headline`, `eyebrow`, `subtitle`, `dotColor`, `breathPeriod`, `ringEvery`.
- source: x2 11.75–15.0 s (`b_5.png`, `b_6.png`, `sheet.png`).
- value: calm, premium end card / slogan beat; website hero headline with a living accent.

## Styles

### style `swiss-reel` — Swiss motion reel
- essence: flat full-bleed colour chapters, one red-orange dot as the hero, technical thin-line overlays (axes, handles, bounding boxes, ticks) and a mono studio HUD; bold grotesk/CJK type; everything precise, centred and demonstrative.
- palette: bg paper `#EDE9E1` (vignette `#CDC9C1`), bg2 red-orange `#FC491D`, bg3 ultramarine `#1F32EE`, bg4 black `#0C0B10`, ink `#1E181E`, paper-ink on dark `#EADED8`, accent dot `#FC491D` (on orange use black/white), accent2 lime `#D2FC36`, HUD grey `#808078`.
- texture / post: light film grain (± 3 lum) and a soft radial vignette (≈ 15 %) on flat fields; no gradients inside shapes; glow only on the particle core. Pixel-style engine: `grain 0.03`, `vignette 0.15`, no posterise.
- typography: **JetBrains Mono** (HUD, captions, numbers; caps, +25 % tracking), **Inter** Black / **Archivo Black** (display words), **Noto Sans SC** Black (CJK display), **Inter** Regular (subtitles).
- motion rules: 60 fps smooth; ≈ 2 s per idea; snappy easeOutExpo/easeOutCubic entrances with small spring overshoots; designed transitions that grow from the hero object (never plain crossfades); background colour changes on every chapter; the accent dot persists across all scenes.
- signature moves: `timeline-bounce-study`, `ball-zoom-flood`, `bezier-handle-graph`, `stepped-diagonal-wipe`, `shape-morph-chain`, `line-open-wipe`, `type-marquee-field`, `slice-assemble-glyph`, `particle-shape-morph`, `core-shockwave-reveal`, `dash-flow-field`, `field-blinds-wipe`, `breathing-end-card`, `reel-chapter-hud`.
- do: keep one accent object; alternate light/dark/colour chapters; label things (sizes, bezier values, N =); centre the subject. don't: use more than 4–5 hues, add drop shadows or 3D lighting to flat shapes, let the HUD compete with the hero (keep it ≤ 60 % opacity).
- source: x2 (video by someone else; analysis only).
