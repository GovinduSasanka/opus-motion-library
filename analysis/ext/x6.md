## Motion techniques

### `scramble-decode` — EXTENDS `typewriter-signature` (new mode `decode` + display sizes)
- look: a title appears as a row of random symbols in the same font, and the real letters lock in left to right while the rest keeps flickering; reversed for the exit (real letters break back into symbols, then fade).
- params: "[ GENERATING SIMULATION NO. 82 ]" 3.65–4.30 s (0.65 s for 32 chars ≈ 20 ms/char); "[ Enter the Simulation ]" 4.73–5.40 s; scramble-out 8.10–8.35 s. Unresolved glyphs re-randomise every 2 frames (15 Hz) from a pool `[]{}|\/#*&%@^<>!` + letters; the brackets resolve first. Resolved front advances linearly; a 3–4-char band just ahead of the front flickers at 60 % opacity.
- build: `resolved = floor(clamp((f - start)/dur) * n)`; char i shows `text[i]` if `i < resolved`, else `pool[hash(i, floor(f/2))]`. Props: `text`, `start`, `durationFrames`, `reseedEvery=2`, `pool`, `direction` (`ltr` | `center-out` | `random`), `out` (scramble-out frame). Works in any font, including pixel blackletter.
- source: x6 3.65–5.40 s and 8.10–8.35 s (`c_scramble.png`, `b_2.4.png`, `b_4.4.png`).
- value: "AI is generating / booting / decrypting" moments; techy title reveals.

### `container-expand` — NEW (transition)
- look: the clicked UI element (a button) grows into a full-screen solid panel that becomes the next screen; on exit the screen shrinks back into a small rectangle and then into a single dot on black (old-TV switch-off, but rectangular).
- params: expand 3.07→3.32 s (0.25 s, easeInQuad): 0.27 W × 0.20 H at the button → 0.45 × 0.40 → 0.82 × 0.80 → full; a 1 px white centre dot stays visible. Collapse 8.35→8.60 s: full → 0.88 W → 0.12 W × 0.12 H → 4 px dot (easeInCubic), then the dot holds 0.25 s on black.
- build: transition presentation with `from` rect (x, y, w, h in %) and colour; interpolate the rect to full frame, fill it with the colour, cross to the next scene's first frame inside the rect at u = 0.9. Collapse = reverse with `to` = dot. Props: `fromRect`, `color`, `dot`, `holdDotFrames`.
- source: x6 3.07–3.32, 8.35–8.83 s (`b_2.4.png`, `b_7.6.png`).
- value: UI-driven flows (click → next screen), product demos, "enter the app" moments.

### `fog-blob` — EXTENDS `blob` transition (new variant `fog`)
- look: from the centre a soft white-misty organic blob opens on black, its wobbly edge feathered like cloud, revealing the map underneath; the misty rim stays visible until the blob passes the frame edges.
- params: 8.85–9.30 s (0.45 s, near-linear growth ≈ 0.3 W per 0.083 s); blob centre (0.58 W, 0.55 H); rim = 0.03 W white band (`#E8ECEC` → transparent, 60 %), edge wobble ±6 % of radius from 3–4 low-frequency sine lobes rotating slowly; inside, the map starts at 120 % brightness and settles.
- build: `blob` with `variant: 'fog'`: radial mask polygon `r(θ) = R(u)·(1 + 0.06·Σ sin(kθ + φk + 0.5u))`; feather 0.03 W via `feGaussianBlur` on the mask; draw a white blurred stroke along the edge. Props: `center`, `wobble`, `rimColor`, `feather`.
- source: x6 8.85–9.30 s (`b_7.6.png`).
- value: "world loads / map reveal / fog of war clears" for games, maps and dashboards.

### `army-formation` — NEW
- look: painterly isometric field with four blocks of tiny unit sprites (infantry blocks, cloaked archer cones, cavalry), each block grouped around a tall banner shield carrying the faction's logo; units bob slightly; volleys of white chevron arrows arc between blocks. Spawn: every block first appears as white placeholder silhouettes, then floods with the faction colour.
- params: 4 factions at N/E/S/W of the field (block ≈ 0.22 W × 0.12 H, 60–150 units in rows on an isometric 2:1 grid, spacing 1.3 % W). Spawn 9.8 s white → colour 10.3–10.8 s (0.5 s cross-fade, staggered 0.1 s per block). Unit bob ±1 px at 1.5 Hz with random phase. Arrows: white 2-stroke chevrons, parabolic arcs 0.6–0.9 s flight. Banner 0.04 W × 0.08 H, slight tilt, pale tint of the faction colour.
- build: SVG or three.js instanced sprites on an iso projection `screen = (x−y)·cos30, (x+y)·sin30`; unit = 3–4-path low-poly silhouette per type; `tint` via a `feColorMatrix` blend from white to faction colour; march = translate the block along a path with per-unit lag (`delay = rowIndex·2 f`). Props: `factions[] {name, color, logo, units, position}`, `spawnAt`, `march[]`, `volleys[]`, `backdrop` (image), `seed`.
- source: x6 9.3–46.9 s (`f_12.png`, `f_16.5.png`, `f_21.png`, `f_25.png`, `b_9.png`).
- value: "AI models / teams / products compete" stories, strategy explainers, market-share battles, game trailers.

### `data-view-flash` — NEW
- look: for 1–5 frames, on the beat, the shot cuts to its "data" version: the same army rendered as flat glyphs (circles, triangles, hexes) in one faction colour on near-black noise, sometimes with an RGB split on the second frame, then cuts straight back. Faction-to-faction shot changes are hidden inside these flashes.
- params: flash lengths 1–5 frames at 30 fps (13.97–14.13 = 5 f, 14.80 = 1 f, 24.77–24.85 = 3 f incl. RGB-split frame, 26.02–26.10 = 3 f); interval 0.60–0.67 s (beat). Background `#0C0C10` with 6 % luminance noise; glyphs flat `#E58682` / `#B391F7` etc., 85 % opacity; RGB split ±8 px red/blue channel offset.
- build: a `Sequence` per beat that swaps the scene for a "glyph render" of the same entities (same positions, each unit replaced by a primitive); for images, approximate with a threshold of the subject's alpha into a tiled hex/dot pattern (`dot-matrix` style `glyph`). Props: `beats[]` or `bpm`, `flashFrames`, `glyphs`, `colors`, `rgbSplit`.
- source: x6 13.8–26.4 s (`b_13.8.png`, `b_24.6.png`).
- value: rhythmic "under the hood / what the AI sees" accents; hides hard cuts on music beats.

### `tactical-map` — NEW
- look: a "HUD OFF" symbolic view of the battle: dark isometric grid, each army a cluster of coloured glyphs (circles = infantry, triangles = archers, hexes = cavalry), thin selection rectangles, faint dashed link lines between commanders and targets, small floating command boxes; the HUD panels stay on top.
- params: bg `#030309`, grid lines 1 px `#1A1A2A` every 4 % W on the iso plane; glyph size 0.6–1.2 % W; selection boxes 1 px white at 70 %; links 1 px dashed white 40 %; entry/exit via horizontal glitch bands (36.13, 36.88, 47.1).
- build: same entity data as `army-formation`, rendered as SVG primitives on the same iso projection (so the toggle is exact). Props: `entities`, `colors`, `grid`, `links`, `selection`.
- source: x6 36.13–37.13, 40.10–40.25, 47.10–47.35 s (`f_36.5.png`, `b_35.8.png`).
- value: abstract strategy diagrams, "simulation view" of any system, data-overlay version of a scene.

### `unit-callouts` — EXTENDS `callout-labels` (new variant `rts-select`)
- look: white 1 px rectangles snap around groups of units, each with a small dark label tag "MOVE" / "ATTACK" above and a short white HP bar; boxes jitter and resize as units move, appearing and disappearing every few frames; a pale ellipse ring with tick marks and a name tag circles a selected army.
- params: box line 1.5 px white 85 %, tag 10 px mono uppercase on `#1E2420` 80 %, HP bar 0.03 W × 3 px; boxes resample every 3–4 frames (stepped); ring = ellipse aspect 0.45, 1 px, `#BFE6F0` 70 %, with a 0.08 W leader line to a "CLAUDE BASTION / IDLE" label (11.17 s).
- build: in `callout-labels` add `variant: 'rts-select'` with `items[] {box, tag, hp}` and `jitterEvery`; ring item `{ellipse, label}`. Props as existing + `stepFrames`.
- source: x6 11.17 s, 37.1–46.9 s (`f_40.5.png`, `f_43.png`).
- value: game HUD look; pointing out actors in any crowd or system diagram.

### `event-feed` — NEW
- look: top-right stack of one-line event chips: `[CHATGPT] archer destroyed by [GEMINI]`, each faction name in its coloured chip, the verb in white mono; newest line pushes in at the top, older lines drop down and fade after ≈ 3 s.
- params: line height 2.2 % H, chip padding 0.3 × 0.6 em, chip colours per faction (see palette), text 13 px mono white; new line: 1-frame dim flash then fade-in 0.15 s, push-down 0.2 s easeOutCubic; max 6 lines.
- build: list of `{t, a, verb, b}` events; for each, `y = index·lineH` with spring on index change; opacity fades after `ttl`. Props: `events[]`, `colors{}`, `maxLines`, `ttlFrames`, `align`.
- source: x6 33–47 s (`c_killfeed.png`, `f_36.5.png`).
- value: live-event tickers (deploys, sales, alerts, game kills) for dashboards and product demos.

### `agent-roster` — NEW
- look: a 2×2 block of agent status cards in the bottom-left: square portrait, name, coloured status chip ("THINKING...", "CRUSHING...", "ON STANDBY"), points "396PT", a token counter "1338470TK" that ticks up, and a thin progress bar under each card. Bottom-right: a row of line icons (Settings, Mute, HUD ON/OFF, About, Pause).
- params: card 0.12 W × 0.06 H, portrait 0.03 W; label 12 px mono; status chip in faction colour; token counter rolls continuously (≈ 30–60 k tokens/s); bars 2 px white 70 %.
- build: compose with `number-counter` (tick mode) for points/tokens; cards slide in from the left (0.3 s, easeOutCubic, 0.08 s stagger). Props: `agents[] {name, portrait, color, status, points, tokens}`, `position`, `controls`.
- source: x6 10.5–47 s (`f_12.png`, `f_40.5.png`).
- value: multi-agent / leaderboard dashboards, AI benchmark videos.

### `agent-log-panel` — NEW
- look: a dark translucent panel on the left with a 1 px white frame, header "TURN: 18/31  ACTION  MANUAL ▷" and icons, scrolling a conversation log: avatar, coloured name chip ("General Claude:"), mono message with @mentions highlighted, "N replies View conversation", collapsible "Gemini Army Reports" rows and "------ COMMUNICATION PHASE ------" dividers; new messages type in and the log scrolls up.
- params: panel 0.25 W × 0.72 H at (0.02 W, 0.04 H); bg `#0E1214` 70 %; text 13 px mono `#E8E8E8`, chips 13 px on faction colour; scroll speed ≈ 1 message per 1.5 s with 0.3 s easeOut; typing 40–60 chars/s.
- build: reuse `typewriter-code` line layout with rich spans; messages array with `{author, color, text, replies?}`; scroll = `translateY(-offset)` with spring per new message. Props: `messages[]`, `header`, `dividers`, `typeSpeed`.
- source: x6 29–47 s (`crop_log.png`, `f_43.png`).
- value: show agent reasoning or chat history next to the action; customer-support or multi-agent demos.

### `versus-roster` — NEW
- look: a row of 4 square portrait cards separated by small "vs", each framed with white L-corner brackets and a small uppercase name above ("GENERAL GROK"); the hovered card scales up and a short mono bio types in below it; the other cards stay small.
- params: cards 0.11 W square at y 0.45 H, gap 0.06 W, "vs" 12 px mono; hover scale 1.0 → 1.3 (0.2 s easeOutBack 1.2) each ≈ 0.8 s in turn; bio 13 px mono, 24 chars/line, typed at ≈ 60 chars/s. Cards first appear as black squares (4.73) and the portraits cut in (5.07).
- build: flex row of `<Img>` cards; `hoverIndex(f)` schedule; bracket corners = 4 small `<path>` L-shapes inset −4 px. Props: `cards[] {name, src, bio}`, `hoverSchedule`, `separator`, `bracketColor`.
- source: x6 4.6–8.1 s (`f_6.5.png`, `b_4.4.png`).
- value: "four contenders" intro for comparisons, tournaments, product line-ups.

### `prompt-wall` — NEW (backdrop)
- look: the blue screen is filled with 20–30 faint wireframe boxes holding fragments of prompt templates ("// ORDERS – TURN 14", "{{aiProvider}}", "RECRUIT YOUR ARMY…"), at 20–35 % opacity, slowly typing and fading; a few lines in the foreground are brighter.
- params: bg `#1206A5`; boxes 1 px `#5C5BA0`, text 12–14 px mono `#615DC9`; 3 depth tiers (opacity 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.45, scale 0.9 / 1 / 1.05); new box every 0.3 s, each lives 2–4 s.
- build: seeded grid of panels, each a `typewriter`-style reveal; overlay-capable (transparent bg option). Props: `color`, `ink`, `snippets[]`, `density`, `seed`.

### `circuit-traces` — NEW (overlay)
- look: a faint glowing PCB pattern (traces with 45° bends ending in round pads) appears on the ground of the map, drawn on, then breathes at low opacity under the armies.
- params: traces 1.5–2 px `#E8FFE0` at 25–40 % with 6 px glow, pads 4–6 px rings; draw-on 0.6 s at 9.2 s; afterwards opacity 0.2–0.35 pulsing at 0.3 Hz; projected on the iso ground plane.
- build: seeded Manhattan/45° random walk paths from a few roots; `strokeDashoffset` draw-on; apply the iso transform to the group. Props: `color`, `density`, `drawFrames`, `projection` (`flat` | `iso`), `seed`.
- source: x6 2.57 s, 9.2–9.5 s, 16–46 s (`f_16.5.png`, `f_43.png`).

## Styles

### style `klein-terminal` — Klein-blue terminal gothic
- essence: a saturated ultramarine terminal screen with pixel-blackletter titles and monospace body text, thin white wireframes with corner brackets, scanlines and RGB glitch — "medieval meets machine".
- palette: bg `#1206A5`, ink (ghost text) `#615DC9`, paper/ui `#FFFFFF`, title `#FFF6E2`, accent (alert/bracket) `#E0503C`, accent2 (dark mode bg) `#030309`, faction chips `#E58682`, `#A9D9D0`, `#CBB8F5`, `#141227`.
- texture / post: scanlines (`scanlines: {gap: 3, opacity: 0.08}`), slight RGB split on titles (`misregister dx 2 dy 0 color #FF2A6D 0.35` + a cyan copy), film grain 0.04, glitch bursts (horizontal band displacement) at scene changes; no paper, no vignette.
- typography: **Jacquard 24** (titles, wrapped in `[ ]`), **IBM Plex Mono** (body, labels, HUD), **Rajdhani** Light (big HUD announcements in uppercase).
- motion rules: 30 fps; text never fades in — it scrambles in (`scramble-decode`) or types; panels expand from the element clicked; hard cuts and 1–5-frame data flashes on the beat (≈ 92 BPM); UI eases easeOutCubic 0.2–0.3 s; hover = scale 1.3 with small overshoot.
- signature moves: `scramble-decode`, `container-expand`, `prompt-wall`, `versus-roster`, `data-view-flash`, `event-feed`, `agent-roster`, `glitch-silhouette`.
- do: keep everything on the blue or near-black; use brackets `[ ]` as the frame motif; keep body text small and monospace. don't: use gradients, rounded cards or soft shadows; mix more than one display font.
- source: x6 (someone else's promo; analysis only).
