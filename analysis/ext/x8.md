## Motion techniques

### `circuit-rebuild` — NEW
- look: a technical assembly that rebuilds itself when the "step" changes: outgoing parts leave quickly (cables break and retract, parts pop off upward/outward and shrink), the camera re-frames, and incoming parts arrive in a fixed order — base pieces slide in, small parts drop from above with a tiny bounce, large props grow up from a floor tile, and cables grow last from the source pin to their target. Shadows travel with each part and fade in on landing.
- params (60 fps effective): teardown 0.12–0.20 s, all parts nearly together (stagger 1–2 f), easeInCubic, scale → 0.3 and fade, exit direction = away from the base (up-right for the sensor). Build 0.5–1.0 s: slide-in 0.35 s easeOutCubic from +0.15 W; drop-in from −0.08 H, 0.15 s easeOutBack(1.3); grow-from-tile scale 0.1 → 1 over 0.22 s easeOutBack(1.2), pivot at the base; part stagger 0.08–0.12 s; cables last, 0.2 s each, 0.1 s apart. Info card text cross-fades 0.15 s at the start.
- build: a scene = list of parts `{id, kind, iso position, size, enter: 'slide'|'drop'|'grow', exit}` + cables. Diff two steps by `id`: parts present in both tween position (easeInOutCubic 0.4 s), parts only in the old step play `exit`, only in the new step play `enter` after `teardownFrames`. Iso projection `sx = (x − y)·cos30·u`, `sy = (x + y)·sin30·u − z·u`; depth sort by `x + y + z`. Props: `steps[] {parts, cables, camera?}`, `stepAt[]`, `teardownFrames`, `stagger`, `enterDefaults`, `palette`.
- source: x8 0.90–1.90, 4.2–6.4, 8.2–8.9, 20.1–20.75, 29.0–30.9, 37.9–38.7, 53.7–54.75 s (`b_0.9.png`, `cb_build1.png`, `cb_swap.png`, `cb_20.png`, `cb_30.png`, `cb_38.png`, `cb_54.png`).
- value: step-by-step product / hardware / architecture explainers ("add a database, then a cache…"), onboarding tours, kit unboxing, any "here is step N" sequence.

### `cable-grow` — EXTENDS `line-draw-on` (new stroke style `cable` + `retract` mode)
- look: thick rounded cables drawn as an outline + colour + thin highlight, growing as a smooth arc from a connector pin to the breadboard; when removed they split into 2–3 pieces that shrink towards their ends and vanish.
- params: cable width ≈ 0.45 % W (outline 0.08 % W `#1C253B`, highlight 30 % white along the upper edge), arc = cubic Bézier rising ≈ 0.12 H above the straight line; grow 0.2 s easeOutQuad, a 2-frame connector-cap pop at the start; retract: 2–3 random cut points, each piece's dash shrinks to 0 in 0.08–0.12 s, easeInQuad. Colours red/yellow/green/blue/black.
- build: in `line-draw-on` add `style: 'cable'` (3 stacked strokes with `stroke-linecap: round`) and `retract: {pieces, frames}` that rewrites the dash array into `pieces` segments shrinking around their own centres. Props: `from`, `to`, `lift`, `color`, `width`, `growFrames`, `retractAt`.
- source: x8 1.65–1.85, 4.3, 5.0–5.8, 8.24–8.37, 31.3–31.8 s (`cb_build1.png`, `cb_swap.png`, `s_led.png`).
- value: connections in diagrams (API → service, sensor → board), wiring explainers, network links that look tactile.

### `auto-frame-camera` — NEW (core helper)
- look: as parts are added or removed the whole scene pans and zooms smoothly so the current assembly fills the scene area with a constant margin; the UI never moves.
- params: re-frame 0.6–0.8 s, easeInOutCubic (measured frame-difference ramp 0.95 → 5.4 over 1.0–1.6 s, then decays); margin ≈ 8 % of the viewport; in this video zoom range 1.0–1.25× (bare board is shown largest, the full project smallest).
- build: compute the projected bounding box of the visible parts for each step, derive `scale = min(vw/bw, vh/bh)·(1 − margin)` and `translate` to centre; interpolate between steps (start at the teardown frame). Hook usable by any multi-step motion. Props: `margin`, `frames`, `easing`, `minScale`, `maxScale`, `anchor`.
- source: x8 1.0–1.8, 54.3–54.75 s (`b_0.9.png`, `cb_54.png`).
- value: keeps any build-up diagram readable without hand-keyed cameras.

### `measure-gizmo` — EXTENDS `sonar-ping` (new `iso` ground mode + measurement line)
- look: a teal dashed line runs from the sensor to a round handle on the obstacle, a navy pill shows the live distance ("48 cm"), two concentric teal ellipses lie on the floor around the selected obstacle, and short orange arcs pulse from the obstacle back towards the sensor (the echo).
- params: dashed line 0.12 % W, dash 6 / gap 5 px at 1080p-equivalent, teal `#2FA493`; handle = white dot with teal ring Ø 0.6 % W; pill `#181B30`, white mono text, radius 50 %, offset above-right of the object; ground rings iso ellipses (aspect 0.5), radii 1.0× and 1.15× the object footprint, 0.25 % W stroke, 70 % and 40 % opacity; echo arcs orange `#E5A24C`, 2–3 arcs, period ≈ 0.6 s; label counts integer cm while dragging (17 → 50 cm over 0.9 s, updated each frame).
- build: `sonar-ping` with `projection: 'iso'` for rings and arcs (ellipse `ry = 0.5·rx`), plus a measurement layer: `distance = |p_obstacle − p_sensor|·unitsToCm`, `label = Math.round(distance) + ' cm'`. Props: `from`, `to` (animated), `unit`, `color`, `labelColor`, `rings`, `echo`.
- source: x8 15.5–20.1, 44–53 s (`crop_sensor.png`, `s_ping.png`, `s_car.png`).
- value: distances, ranges, signal strength or "selected object" highlights in iso diagrams; product UI demos.

### `iso-actuators` — NEW
- look: small isometric hardware that comes alive when "running": an LED glows on/off, a DC-motor fan spins (blades replaced by a motion-blur disc at speed), a servo horn sweeps through an angle with a live "57°" pill, a traffic light switches green/yellow/red, a toy car drives back and forth along an iso axis.
- params: LED blink 1 s period (on 0.5 s), glow radius 2.5× LED, `#FF5A4A` at 60 %; fan PWM ramp 0 → 255 in steps of 5 every 40 ms (from the code), blade rotation ∝ duty, blur disc above ≈ 8 rev/s; servo 0 → 180° at 12 ms/° (≈ 2.2 s per sweep), horn pivots on the iso top face; car moves ±0.08 W along the iso x axis, easeInOutSine, period ≈ 3 s, with the ground ring following; traffic light thresholds > 30 cm green, 15–30 yellow, < 15 red.
- build: each actuator is a small SVG component with an iso-projected pivot (`rotate` in the top-face plane = rotate then apply the iso matrix); drive values from a shared `signal(t)` so the code highlight, the serial log and the hardware agree. Props per actuator: `kind` (`led` | `motor` | `servo` | `trafficLight` | `car`), `signal` (function or keyframes), `color`, `label`.
- source: x8 1.9–4.2, 20.75–29, 30.9–37.9, 38.7–53.7 s (`grid_a.png`, `grid_b.png`, `s_servo.png`, `s_car.png`).
- value: IoT / maker / robotics content; "the code drives the hardware" explanations; playful product UIs.

### `code-run-sync` — EXTENDS `typewriter-code` (new `highlight` schedule + `console` pane + run state)
- look: a light-theme code editor card with line numbers; pressing Run turns the play button into a red stop button, a serial-monitor pane opens below the code ("● SERIAL MONITOR RUNNING"), shows "Compiling sketch… Uploading to Arduino Uno… Done uploading." and then appends log lines that scroll up; a pale teal band steps from line to line through `loop()` in time with the hardware; numeric literals sit on small teal chips.
- params: editor card `#F5EFE1`, border `#D7CCBA`, line height 2.2 % H at 3456 px width; highlight band `#21A69A` at 12 %, steps every 0.25–0.5 s (follows the `delay()` values, scaled); serial pane ≈ 40 % of the card height, new line every 0.1–0.6 s with 0.12 s scroll easeOutCubic; status dot teal; upload phase ≈ 1.2 s.
- build: in `typewriter-code` add `theme: 'paper'`, `highlight: {lines[], everyFrames}` (or keyframes), `console: {lines: (t) => string[], status}`, `runAt`. Props as listed + `chips` (wrap numbers in rounded rects).
- source: x8 13–20, 22–29, 40–53 s (`crop_code.png`, `s_sensor.png`).
- value: tutorials, API demos and dev-tool promos where code and result must stay in sync.

### `lesson-carousel` — NEW (ui)
- look: a bottom strip of cards, each with an isometric thumbnail of the part and a label "7. READ A SENSOR ›"; completed lessons get a teal circular check badge on the top-right corner; the active card has a 2 px teal outline and bold label; arrow buttons scroll the strip by three cards with a smooth slide; a cursor clicks the next card.
- params: card 0.115 W × 0.06 H, gap 0.008 W, radius 0.4 % W; badge Ø 1.4 % W teal `#21A69A` with white check, pops in with scale 0 → 1.15 → 1 over 6 f; strip scroll 0.3 s easeInOutCubic (6.7–7.0); active outline fades in 0.1 s.
- build: flex row in a clipped container, `translateX = -index·(card + gap)` with `spring` or `interpolate`; cursor sprite moves on eased paths and plays a 3-frame press scale (0.92). Props: `items[] {label, thumb}`, `activeAt[]`, `completed[]`, `scrollAt[]`, `accent`, `cursor`.
- source: x8 0.9, 6.7–7.0, 20.1, 29, 37.9, 53.7 s (`grid_a.png`, `grid_b.png`).
- value: course / playlist / step navigation in app promos; chapter selector at the bottom of an explainer.

### `inspector-popover` — NEW (ui)
- look: a small floating property card next to the selected object: title "BRICK WALL" + close ×, one-line description, a segmented chip group ("Car | Box | Ball | Wall") with the active chip filled navy, a teal slider with its value ("48 cm"), a toggle "Drive back and forth while running", and a "+ ZOOM TO IT" button with a teal "or drag it in the scene" hint. It fades/scales in when the object is clicked and fades out on step change.
- params: card 0.105 W wide, radius 0.6 % W, bg `#F7F2E8`, shadow 0 6 px 18 px `#7A6A5050`; in: opacity 0 → 1 and scale 0.96 → 1 over 0.15 s; out: opacity only over 0.12 s (20.2–20.3, 53.8–53.9); slider knob moves in sync with the dragged object; chip switch = 1-frame swap.
- build: HTML-in-SVG (`foreignObject`) or absolutely positioned div; props `title`, `description`, `chips[]`, `activeChip`, `slider {label, value(t), min, max, unit}`, `toggle`, `anchor`.
- source: x8 15.5–20.2, 44–53.8 s (`crop_sensor.png`, `s_car.png`).
- value: product UI demos, design-tool and configurator promos, "tweak a parameter" explainers.

## Camera

- One fixed screen; only the scene viewport has a camera. It is a 2D pan/zoom over an isometric world (no perspective, no rotation).
- Re-frames on every step change (`auto-frame-camera`): 0.6–0.8 s easeInOutCubic, zoom 1.0–1.25×. The end reset zooms back in on the bare board.
- Small UI controls under the viewport (undo, redo, −, +, info) hint at manual zoom, but the recording never uses them.

## Styles

### style `iso-workbench` — Isometric drafting-paper workbench
- essence: clean isometric technical illustration on warm drafting paper: outlined flat-shaded objects, soft offset shadows, a faint iso grid and PCB-trace ornaments, with cream UI cards and one teal accent.
- palette: bg `#F4ECDB`, paper2 `#F7F2E4`, grid `#E8DCC6`, ink `#1C253B`, text `#232631`, muted `#8A8273`, card `#F5EFE1`, border `#D7CCBA`, ornament `#C9B79C`, shadow `#B8A58C`, accent (teal) `#21A69A`, accent2 (echo orange) `#E5A24C`, object blue `#4D588A`, object red `#D63A33`.
- texture / post: no grain, no blur. Isometric grid = two families of 1 px lines at ±30° plus faint verticals every 4 % W, `#E8DCC6` at 70 %; margin ornaments = orthogonal/45° traces with small ring pads, `#C9B79C`, 1.5 px. Objects: 2–3 px `#1C253B` outline (`stroke-linejoin: round`), three tones per material (top = base +12 % L, left = base, right = base −18 % L), 1–2 white specular ticks on edges. Cast shadow = the object's footprint polygon offset (+0.6 % W, +0.5 % H), `#B8A58C` at 45 %, `feGaussianBlur` 2 px. As a pixel-only style in `src/styles` (for non-iso motions): `posterize 6` → `edges` `#1C253B` width 2 → `tint #F4ECDB multiply 0.25` → paper overlay `{color: '#F4ECDB', amount: 0.12}`; it gives the outlined flat look but not the iso geometry.
- typography: **Barlow Semi Condensed** Medium caps tracked +20 % (titles, labels), **Barlow** (body), **IBM Plex Mono** (code, values, pills).
- motion rules: 60 fps, UI-like timing: exits 0.12–0.2 s easeIn, entrances 0.15–0.35 s easeOut / easeOutBack(1.2–1.3) with 0.08–0.12 s stagger, camera 0.6–0.8 s easeInOutCubic; no cuts — everything changes in place; values (distance, angle, %) update every frame in mono pills; the code highlight and the hardware share one clock.
- signature moves: `circuit-rebuild`, `cable-grow`, `auto-frame-camera`, `measure-gizmo`, `iso-actuators`, `code-run-sync`, `lesson-carousel`, `inspector-popover`.
- do: keep strict 2:1 isometric angles, one accent colour, outlines on every object, shadows down-right. don't: mix perspective with iso, add gradients or glows (except LED glow), or animate the UI chrome.
- source: x8 (someone else's app recording; analysis only).
