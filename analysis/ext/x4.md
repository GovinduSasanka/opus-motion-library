## Motion techniques

### `creator-hand` — NEW (character / overlay-capable)
- look: a giant "real-world" hand and forearm in a chunky red ribbed sweater enters from a frame edge and handles things inside the illustrated world: unpins a card, carries it, drops it into a machine, holds a rubber stamp. It is drawn in the same illustration style but at a much larger scale than the world's characters (a scale-break that says "you, the user, drive this"). It moves in slow, soft arcs, is held still between actions, and animates on twos in the first shot.
- params: forearm enters from the right/top-right, wrist at ≈ 0.75 W; sleeve cuff ≈ 0.10 H tall with rib lines; motion path card: (0.61 W, 0.34 H) at 0 s → (0.49 W, 0.47 H) at 2 s → (0.55 W, 0.72 H) at 4.2 s (soft S-curve, easeInOutSine), i.e. ≈ 0.1 W/s; pickup = 0.3 s pull away from the board with a 4 % scale-up (closer to camera); release = fingers open over 2 drawings, card falls with easeInQuad. Cadence: frame differences alternate large/small (3.48/0.49 …) for the hand shot → **on twos** at 24 fps; later shots on ones.
- build: SVG/PNG puppet: sleeve + hand as 2–3 layers (`upper arm`, `cuff`, `hand` with 2–3 grip poses as sprites) with an IK-free rig: the hand follows a keyframed path, the sleeve is a tapered quad from the off-screen anchor to the wrist (so it stretches naturally). Optional `onTwos` (quantise time to 2 frames). Props: `from` (edge), `path` (keyframes), `pose` keyframes (`open | pinch | grip | point`), `prop` (`card | stamp | src`), `sleeveColor`, `skin`, `onTwos`.
- source: x4 0.0–4.5, 7.0–7.5, 15.8–17.7 s (`b_a.png`, `t_push.png`, `t_stamp.png`, `cad/`).
- value: "the user's input enters the product" metaphor for promos; tutorial hands; anything where a big hand acts on a small world.

### `device-lid-reveal` — EXTENDS `browser-mockup` (new `device: 'laptop'` with a lid-open intro)
- look: a closed silver laptop on the desk opens: the lid rises from flat toward the camera, the dark screen gains the app image as it comes up, the keyboard appears below; the lid settles at ≈ 105° with a tiny overshoot.
- params: 4.79→5.33 s (≈ 13 frames @24, easeOutCubic with ≈ 3° overshoot); screen content fades from `#1A1A1A` to the image between 30 % and 70 % open; lid outline 2 px ink, aluminium `#CBAE93` (warm-lit), keyboard keys dark grey.
- build: 2.5D: lid as a quad whose top edge moves up (projected `scaleY = sin(angle)` with perspective skew), screen image mapped with CSS `matrix3d` / SVG `<foreignObject>` + transform; base as a static trapezoid. Props: `src` (screen content), `openAt`, `frames`, `angle`, `color`.
- source: x4 4.75–5.40 s (`t_lid.png`).
- value: product promos that start "on the desk"; any app reveal.

### `screen-portal-push` — NEW (transition; relates to `zoom`)
- look: the camera pushes into a device's screen until the screen's content fills the frame; the bezel slides out past the edges, and the content (which was a small picture on the screen) becomes the full-resolution next scene. A hand can reach "through" the screen during the push, crossing from the room layer into the screen world.
- params: 6.0→7.0 s push (screen width 0.50 W → 1.15 W, easeInOutCubic), bezel off-frame by 7.0; crossfade from the low-res screen image to the full scene 7.2→7.5 (the new shot is reframed: a street lamp and buildings appear at the edges, i.e. the full scene is wider than the screen crop); a slight warm glare on the screen surface fades as it fills.
- build: two layers: `room` (with the device) and `inner` scene. Transform both with the same camera `scale(s)` about the screen centre; the inner scene is drawn clipped to the screen quad until `s` makes the quad exceed the frame; then remove the clip and crossfade to `inner` rendered at native framing. Props: `screenQuad` (4 points), `inner` (scene/src), `pushFrames`, `crossfadeFrames`, `glare`.
- source: x4 6.0–7.5 s (`t_push.png`).
- value: "enter the app / enter the story" transitions; product demo openers; any device-to-content handoff.

### `window-glow-sequence` — NEW (overlay-capable; relates to `city-skyline` window flicker)
- look: in a still illustrated building, the windows light up one after another with a warm interior glow (the glass turns golden, a soft bloom spills on the brickwork), as if the machine inside has been switched on; small tick marks flicker above the roof.
- params: 8.3–9.5 s, 6 windows, stagger ≈ 0.2 s (left → right), each fades in over ≈ 0.15 s from the base glass colour to `#F2C66E` with a 6–10 px bloom at ≈ 40 %; one window flickers once (off 2 frames) before settling.
- build: an overlay on any image: `windows: [{x, y, w, h, shape: 'rect'|'arch'}]` in normalised coords; each = a clipped warm gradient (`#FFE7A8` centre → `#E9A94A` edge) with `mix-blend-mode: screen` + blurred copy for bloom; stagger and optional flicker seed. Props: `windows`, `color`, `stagger`, `fadeFrames`, `flicker`, `order` (`ltr | random | bottom-up`).
- source: x4 8.3–9.5 s (`t_hopper.png`).
- value: "it's alive / powering up / open for business" beats on any building image (factories, stores, houses, city shots).

### `action-ticks` — EXTENDS `emote-pop` (new `ticks` icon set)
- look: 3–5 short curved ink strokes radiate from a point (above the hopper, beside the boy's head, by the stamp) and flicker on and off to show action, sound or joy (manga "emanata").
- params: strokes 8–14 px long, 2 px warm ink `#3E2A20`, fan of ≈ 60°, appear for 3–4 frames, re-draw with a slightly different angle every 2–3 frames, 2–4 cycles.
- build: add `ticks` to `emote-pop`'s icon set: seeded short arcs around an anchor, regenerated on twos. Props: `anchor`, `count`, `spread`, `cycles`.
- source: x4 8.3–10.3, 15.9–17.6, 18.6–19.5 s.
- value: tiny life cues on otherwise still illustrations.

### `hand-stamp-approve` — EXTENDS `stamp-slam` (new `tool: 'hand-stamp'`, `mark: 'check'`)
- look: a hand holding a wooden-handled red rubber stamp hovers with a little wobble, rises (anticipation), slams down on a ticket with a tiny squash of the rubber, holds and rocks once to "ink" it, then lifts and swings away, revealing a green check mark printed on the paper.
- params: hover/anticipation 15.8–16.3 (rise ≈ 0.05 H, tilt −10°), slam 16.3→16.4 (2–3 frames, easeInQuad), contact squash scaleY 0.92 for 2 frames, hold-rock ±3° 16.4–17.2, lift 17.25 (easeOutCubic, 0.25 s) and swing up-right; check mark pops in the frame the stamp leaves (green `#2E8B3A`, stroke ≈ 6 px, inside a small white paper slip). Action ticks around the stamp during hover.
- build: `stamp-slam` with a `tool` layer (handle + red rubber + optional `creator-hand`) and a `mark` layer (`check | cross | text | src`) revealed on lift; timing presets `slamFrames`, `holdFrames`, `liftFrames`. Props: `tool`, `mark`, `markColor`, `target` (x, y), `hold`.
- source: x4 15.79–17.70 s (`t_stamp.png`, `f_16.6.png`).
- value: "approved / verified / passed review" moments in product and process explainers.

### `swing-doors-reveal` — NEW (prop transition)
- look: a pair of wooden half-doors (saloon/stable style, Z-braced planks, iron hinges) in front of the subject swing open toward the camera in a few frames, revealing the character behind, then rock and settle.
- params: open 18.21→18.30 s (≈ 3 frames @24!) to ≈ 70° each, easeOutBack (overshoot ≈ 10°), 2 damped oscillations over 0.5 s; doors ≈ 0.27 W wide each, hinges at the outer posts.
- build: each door = an image/SVG panel with `scaleX = cos(angle)` about its hinge edge plus a perspective skew (`skewY ± angle·0.15`) and darkening of the inner face (`brightness 1 → 0.8`); oscillation `angle = A·(1 − e^{−kt}·cos ωt)`. Props: `doorSrc` (or procedural planks), `openAt`, `angle`, `overshoot`, `damping`, `color`.
- source: x4 18.20–18.75 s (`t_gate.png`).
- value: a playful reveal of a product, a winner or a "you're in" result; also works as a transition (doors fill the frame, open on the next scene).

### `conveyor-flow` — NEW
- look: from the factory's open doors, identical products (cassettes) come out one after another onto a belt and ride it toward the camera / lower right; they grow with perspective as they approach and keep an even spacing; belt rollers turn.
- params: one item every ≈ 0.6 s; travel along the belt ≈ 3 s to leave frame; item scale 0.4 → 1.0 along the path (perspective), speed constant in world space (accelerating on screen, ≈ easeInQuad in screen units); 5–6 items visible; doors open 19.7–20.0 first.
- build: path = 2–3 point polyline in perspective (or a cubic); item `u_i = (t − t0 − i·gap)/travel`; position = path(u), scale = lerp(sNear, sFar, depth(u)); draw back to front; roller dashes move with `strokeDashoffset`. Props: `itemSrc` (or built-in `cassette`, `box`, `card`), `path`, `gapFrames`, `travelFrames`, `perspective`, `count`.
- source: x4 19.62–24.12 s (`b_d.png`).
- value: "output / production / shipping" metaphors; showing many generated items (videos, orders, builds) leaving a system.

### `tv-wall-switch-on` — NEW (relates to `thumbnail-mosaic`)
- look: a cosy shelf wall holding five retro CRT TVs (3 above, 2 below) with dark screens; they switch on one at a time with a quick CRT warm-up (bright grey flash, slight bulge, then the picture fades up) and each plays its own looping clip, so the wall fills with life.
- params: switch-on order top-left → top-middle → top-right → bottom-left → bottom-right at 24.27, 24.60, 24.85, 25.20, 25.43 s (stagger ≈ 0.25–0.35 s); warm-up ≈ 4 frames: screen `#3A3C36` → light grey flash (1 frame, ≈ 70 % white) → image at 60 % with a soft vignette → 100 % over 0.2 s; screens keep a slight inner vignette and warm cast; clips then swap content occasionally (a cut inside a screen at ≈ 26.9 s).
- build: grid of `screen` rects (normalised quads over a background image or procedural TV frames); each screen = clipPath + `<Video>`/image + an overlay for warm-up (`opacity` keyframes + `brightness` flash + scanlines at 8 % + vignette). Props: `screens[]` (`{quad, src}`), `order`, `stagger`, `warmupFrames`, `scanlines`, `background` (src or procedural shelf).
- source: x4 24.12–29.95 s (`t_tv.png`, `b_e.png`).
- value: showcasing many outputs (videos, testimonials, products) in one warm composition; social-proof walls.

### `storybook-end-card` — EXTENDS `end-card-credits` (new `cta` button + title blur-in over a parallax pull-back)
- look: wide establishing shot of the hero building with a slow pull-back and gentle parallax; a solid red CTA button sits low in the frame from the first frame; the title fades and sharpens in at the top in a soft display serif; nothing else.
- params: pull-back ≈ 3 %/s with a slight truck right; foreground pieces (left/right houses, street lamp) slide in from the edges faster than the background (parallax ratio ≈ 2.5×); CTA visible from 29.95 (no animation); title 31.70→32.00 (opacity 0 → 1, blur 8 px → 0, y +6 px → 0, easeOutCubic). Layout per Overview > Fonts.
- build: `end-card-credits` with `layout: 'storybook'`: `title`, `cta: {text, color, textColor}`, background = `ken-burns` (or x5's `multiplane-dolly` when layered). Props: `title`, `cta`, `titleAt`, `font`, `bg`.
- source: x4 29.95–33.50 s (`t_title.png`, `b_f.png`, `f_32.0.png`).
- value: calm, branded endings with a single call to action (GitHub, download, sign up).

## Styles

### style `storybook-gouache` — Storybook gouache (cosy illustrated promo)
- essence: warm hand-painted gouache illustration with thin warm-brown ink lines, sunbeams through windows, terracotta and cream tones, chibi characters, and a big friendly "creator hand" that reaches into the little world. Close to the library's `watercolor` style but more opaque, warmer, with ink lines and a stronger orange/cream grade.
- palette: bg warm cream `#F0DBB0`, wall `#DE9D61`, paper `#FDEBCB`, ink `#3E2A20`, wood `#B88B5D`, brick `#C88760`, sky `#7AA8C9` → `#A2C1D2`, cloud `#E1D7BF`, foliage `#41575B`, accent (sweater / stamp / CTA) `#D2714E` / `#DF5142`, accent2 (window glow) `#F2C66E`, title ink `#261F3D`.
- texture / post: gouache paper grain (fine `feTurbulence` multiply at 5–8 %), soft bloom on sun shafts and lit windows, warm grade (+ temperature, lifted blacks ≈ `#2A1E18`), light vignette 10 %. Pixel-style engine: `posterize 18` → `edges` ink `#3E2A20` 1 px at 50 % → paper overlay `#F3E6CC` 8 % → warm grade → vignette 0.1.
- typography: **Young Serif** or **Fraunces** (SemiBold, soft) for titles; **Fraunces** / **Literata** for CTA and captions; no UI chrome.
- motion rules: 24 fps; hero props/hands on twos for a hand-made feel, environment loops on ones; slow arcs (easeInOutSine) for the hand; snappy reveals (doors in 3 frames, stamp slam in 2–3 frames) with overshoot; hard cuts between shots of 2.5–6 s; one in-world portal transition (screen push) to enter the story.
- signature moves: `creator-hand`, `device-lid-reveal`, `screen-portal-push`, `window-glow-sequence`, `action-ticks`, `hand-stamp-approve`, `swing-doors-reveal`, `conveyor-flow`, `tv-wall-switch-on`, `storybook-end-card`.
- do: keep everything warm and soft; tell the product as a physical process (idea in → approved → shipped → watched); give the user a hand in frame. don't: use UI screenshots or flat vector icons inside the world, cool/neon colours, or busy text — a title and one button at the end are enough.
- source: x4 (video by someone else; imagery likely AI-generated; analysis only).
